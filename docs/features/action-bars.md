# Action bars and cooldowns

What Vanguard does here, in full. This page is part of the behaviour spec; the [README](../../README.md) has the overview and [docs/README.md](../README.md) lists every page.

## Action bars

Each button shows its key in short form: S, C and A for Shift, Ctrl and Alt, Cmd for the Mac's Command key, then the key (S1 for Shift-1, M4 for mouse button 4, WU and WD for the wheel, Sp for Space, Bs for Backspace, PU and PD for Page Up and Down, Ins, Del, Hm and End, Cap for Caps Lock, N1 and N+ for the numpad); any other key keeps its own name, on one line cut at the button's edge. A button whose action is an item you wear (a trinket, say) gets a thin ring just inside its edge in the theme's good colour, as the game marks it, and loses it when the item comes off (not under Masque, which skins the buttons itself). Range and power tint (Red when out of range applies at once), plus restyled pet, stance and extra action buttons and a vehicle exit button (on a flight it asks to land at the next flight point, as the game's own leave button does). The restyle keeps what the game lights up by state: the active stance, the pet's attack flash and the auto-cast mark. Their hotkey and count texts take the theme's small font, as the action bars' do. The vehicle exit button is a 36 unit square that widens to its label when a translation or a larger Text size needs the room. Its default spot sits beside action bar 1's right end and moves out with it at button sizes above 38 (a position you dragged it to stays). The game's cooldown numbers on the buttons use the theme's bold font, in the smaller aura-timer size on buttons under 30 units, so a minute count stays inside the icon. The possess bar's two buttons, shown while you mind-control, get the same restyle. The pet and stance bars sit in spots of their own at the bottom left, above the chat and clear of your bars (the game's own spot for them lies over the bar stack); move them with `/vg unlock`, or turn that off under Action bars to leave them to Edit Mode.

- The bottom bars stack with bar 1 on top and no gap between the rows; turning on another bottom bar adds it underneath. Each row takes its bar's real height, so a bar with bigger buttons or more rows pushes the ones above it up, and a bar you scaled down from its handle pulls them down onto it, right away when you change it. A position you've dragged is kept; `/vg reset all` puts the bars back in this order.
- Empty slots stay hidden until you drag a spell or item, so a new character sees no empty squares, and they take no mouse, so clicks and right-drag camera turns over blank bar space reach the game. A slot that fills or empties in combat (bar 1 paging into a form) shows or hides at once, but takes or releases the mouse only when combat ends; its key still works.
- Hover a button for its action's tooltip, in the game's default spot; it follows the page bar 1 is on, and an empty slot shows none.
- Bar 1 shows the abilities the game gives you in a vehicle, while you mind-control (possess), on a quest's override bar and in a temporary shapeshift (a quest transform). It reads those pages from the client (the pages 16, 18 and 17 on the current engine, kept apart from bars 6 to 8), and falls back to those numbers when the client does not say.
- In a pet battle on retail the bars hide and Vanguard's keys step aside, so the number keys reach the game's pet battle abilities; both come back when the battle ends.
- The game's self-cast, focus-cast and mouseover-cast options work on these buttons as on the game's own.
- Keybind mode (`/vg keybind`, or **Keybind mode** under Action bars): hover a button and press a key, the middle or a side mouse button, or roll the wheel (with Shift, Ctrl or Alt held if you like, or Cmd on a Mac) to bind it; the key replaces the button's keys, is saved in the game's own key bindings (for your account or this character, whichever you use), and shows on the button at once. The Screenshot key still takes a screenshot and binds nothing, as in the game's own key binder. A tooltip over the button shows its keys. Backspace or Delete clears them. A key that did something else (Move Forward, another button) is taken, and chat says from what. A key the game refuses ("UNKNOWN can't be bound." in chat and on the banner) leaves the button's keys as they were, and a key with no name is ignored. The banner's second line repeats the last result (in the warning colour when a key was taken), so you needn't watch chat. While the mode is on a click binds instead of casting (left and right click bind nothing, as in the game). A modifier pressed alone does nothing, a key pressed away from the buttons goes on to the game, and Escape or the banner's **Done** button leaves (the banner widens to show its instruction whole at larger Text sizes and in longer languages). Empty slots and faded bars show while it is on. It works out of combat only and ends when a fight starts.
- Your keys (**Action bars**, the **Your keys** tab): a drawn QWERTY keyboard and the mouse's middle, side and wheel buttons, showing what every key does now. A key bound to one of Vanguard's buttons shows the icon of the spell, macro or item on it with a short key name; a key bound to something else shows only its name in the accent colour; a free key is muted. Hover a key for its full name and what it does. The None, Shift, Ctrl and Alt tabs switch the keys shown, and follow the modifier you hold (letting go goes back to None). Under the keyboard, the tab's bound keys are listed (eight lines; when there are more keys the last line says "and N more"). It only reads, so it stays open in combat and works on every client. AZERTY and QWERTZ keyboards show the same picture by the keys' names. On WoW Forever the card also has **Set up keys** (opens key setup, below) and **Put everything back**, and a key that key setup changed says so in its tooltip: right-click it and confirm to put back what that key did before.
- Counts, icons and usability follow what you carry and wear (bags, equipment), your form, macro edits and spell charges (the count the client itself prints, so a spell with charges shows them), so a stack you use up or a form that changes an icon updates at once.
- Each bar has its own number of buttons, buttons per row, button size, and visibility: Always, On mouseover (seen only while the mouse is over it), In combat, Hidden, or When checked below: a row of conditions under it (Combat, Mouseover, Group, Raid, Instance, Vehicle, Resting; beside the caption, or on lines of their own below it when they don't fit there), and the bar shows while any checked one holds (with Mouseover checked it fades in under the mouse the rest of the time). The game itself shows and hides the bar on those conditions, in combat too; In an instance is re-checked whenever you change zone (after a fight, if you are in one). While you drag a spell or item, bars on mouseover or on conditions show too (the latter only when the drag starts out of combat).
- Hidden bars keep their keys: the bar isn't drawn, but a bound key still casts, like a keybind-only bar. A Hidden bottom bar keeps its row in the stack (leaving a gap), because bars can't be re-stacked during combat.
- The right-edge bars (4 and 5) start off; turned on, they show on mouseover. Profiles that already had them on keep them on Always. Bar 5 sits just inside bar 4 and follows its width (button size, buttons per row), so the two never overlap; a spot you dragged it to stays.
- With the Masque addon installed, Masque skins the buttons instead of Vanguard: its options list four Vanguard groups (Action bars; Pet, stance and extra buttons; Buffs and debuffs, with the weapon enchants; Target and focus auras), each with its own skin. Vanguard bundles nothing of Masque; without it nothing changes.

## Key setup

On WoW Forever only (a retail client shows no Set up keys button and writes nothing). Key setup is a window of three steps around a drawn keyboard, opened from **Set up keys** on the Your keys card, and once by itself at the login after first-run setup ends (when setup and What's new are out of the way; a later login if not).

- **Step 1, Your keys.** "Set up recommended keys for your Warrior?", with **Your role** (Levelling, or the class's roles such as Arms, Fury and Tank; a tank or healer in a group starts on that role) and **My mouse has side buttons (4 and 5)**. **Show me** goes on; **Keep my keys** closes it, and chat says once that nothing changed and where to open it again. With a bar addon loaded (Bartender4, Dominos, ElvUI and the others the Other addons card knows), Vanguard's action bars off, or the game's gamepad interface on, step 1 says why key setup changes nothing and offers only **Close**.
- **Step 2, Here's what changes.** The keyboard and mouse with each proposed key drawn in full with its new icon, and every other key faint as it is now. **Keys with** switches the None, Shift, Ctrl and Alt keys; **Bar page** (warriors, druids and rogues) shows keys 1 to 6 on each stance, form or stealth page; **Show what comes later** greys in the spells the character will learn from a trainer (talents only once taken). Hover a key for what it does now and what it will do. With the pointer over the keyboard, pressing a key lights it, switches to its tab and says what it will do; nothing is cast, Escape still closes the window, and in a fight the keyboard lets keys go. Under it, the changes on the tab and page shown are listed (five lines; "and N more" past that).
- **Step 3, Ready.** Every change in words, each with a tick box ("F: Shield Bash added to your bar", "1: Mortal Strike, and Heroic Strike moves to bar 3", "`: Battle Stance"), and the keys that stay as they are, with why (a macro, an item or a mount on the button, bar 3 full, a bar that is off, another key on that button, a button that already has two keys). Untick a change to leave that key alone. When your keys are shared by all your characters (the game's account key set), it says so, with **Change keys for**: **Only this character** (picked to start with) or **All my characters**. **Apply** applies the ticked changes as below and closes the window; when Apply is refused (a fight, your cursor holding something, the game's key windows open) the window stays and chat says why.
- **Back** returns a step, and **Not now** closes it from steps 2 and 3. A fight closes the window, and it comes back on its step when the fight ends; opened during a fight, it opens after it. It won't open while the game's Settings, Quick Keybind or Click Casting window is open.

### The class layouts

Each class has a layout: what each key means, as a short list of spells written best first. One rule picks every key: **each key takes the first spell on its list that the character knows and that no earlier key on the same bar page has already taken**, so the keys grow with the character (a level-1 warrior's key 1 is Heroic Strike; with Mortal Strike it is Mortal Strike, and Heroic Strike moves to key 4). A role only adds the spells marked for it (a Fury warrior's F is Pummel, a tank's T is Taunt). The keys:

- **1 to 6**: the class's core spells, on bar 1, one set per stance, form or stealth page for warriors, druids and rogues (a shaman's 7 and 8 too, for spells Forever adds).
- **F, T, G, X and Ctrl-X**: interrupt, crowd control (a tank's taunt), a short cooldown or Shoot, a defensive, and a heal on yourself or your pet; **Mouse 4 and 5** (Shift-X and Shift-T without side buttons): a gap closer or escape, and a second utility. These are bar 2's buttons 1 to 7, the same in every stance.
- **Shift-W, Shift-A, Shift-D and Shift-Space**: long buffs, conjures and stealth, on bar 3's buttons 1 to 4.
- **`, Shift-` and Ctrl-`**: the stance bar's forms (by where each sits on the game's stance bar), or Pet Attack and Pet Follow for hunters and warlocks.
- C, R, V, Y, K, B, H, Z and keys 7 to = (9 to = for a shaman) are left to you.

A spell WoW Forever added or changed whose ID isn't confirmed yet (Victory Rush, Lacerate, Holy Strike, the shaman Calls and others) ships as an empty key, never a guess; `/vg probe` lists every layout's spell IDs with the names the client gives them.

## Key setup: how presets are applied

- **What a preset writes.** Bars 1 to 3 only: bar 1 (and its stance, form and stealth pages), bar 2 and bar 3, and only the buttons Vanguard's action bars draw (a bar that is off, or a button past the bar's button count, is never written, and nothing turns it on). For each key, in order: when its button is on bar 2 or 3 and the spell is already on bar 2 or 3, the key is bound to that button; otherwise the spell goes on the key's own button when it is empty; when that button holds a spell you know, that spell moves to a free slot of bar 3's 5 to 12 first (bar 3 full or off: the key stays as it was), and a later key in the same preset may still bind to it there. A key for a bar 1 button always stays on its own button, since bar 1 changes with your stance, and one key may fill its button on several stance pages. A macro, an item, a mount or anything else on the button is never touched, and that key stays as it was. A spell you don't know yet gets no key. A button that already has two other keys takes no third, and that key stays as it was. Stance and pet keys bind to the game's own stance and pet buttons.
- **When it runs.** Applying keys and putting them back are both refused in combat, while the game restricts add-ons (an encounter, a Mythic+ run or a PvP match), while the game's Settings, Quick Keybind or Click Casting window or Vanguard's keybind mode is open, and while your cursor holds something: chat says why, nothing changes, and nothing is queued for later. The keys are saved in the game's own key bindings, in the key set you use; with "Only this character" your character first gets its own key set, as the game's own switch makes it. A character that already has its own keys is not switched back to account keys; chat says to do that in the game's key bindings.
- **All or nothing.** If the game refuses a key or a spell (a button that doesn't take it), blocks one of the changes, or something goes wrong, everything that Apply changed goes back (after the fight, if one began) and chat says no key changed. A button you changed during that fight stays as you set it. A button that can't be put back then (your cursor holds something) is kept for "Put everything back", and chat says so.
- **Putting back.** Vanguard records what each key and button held before it first changed them; if you changed one yourself before Vanguard changed it again, what you set is what goes back. Putting back one key also puts back the spells it placed or moved, to what that key found there (a button another key still uses keeps its spell); putting everything back restores every key and every spell it changed on this character's bars for that key set. A key or a button you changed since stays as you set it, and chat names those keys; a key the game refuses to put back stays recorded, and chat names it too. Keys set in your account key set and put back on another character go back there at once; the spells they moved on every other character's bars go back when you next log in as that character (unless that key was set again since). Putting back is refused while the game uses the other key set (you switched between account and character keys since), and chat says so. If Vanguard's saved settings don't load one session (the settings guard), the account's key record comes back from this character's copy.

## Cooldown gaps

A small, display-only row of icons for what the game's Cooldown Manager misses, off until you tick "Gaps row" on the Cooldowns page (a reload applies it). It adds to the game's manager and never replaces it, in the same square-icon look with the theme's timer font, in a spot you can move with `/vg unlock`. It shows the on-use trinkets you wear, items you choose while you carry them (listed on the Cooldowns page's Gaps card, where you drag an item or spell onto the add row or type its name or ID and press Add item or Add spell, and × takes one off; the card says to switch the row on while it is off. Or `/vg gaps add <item>`: a link, an ID or a name; `remove <item>`, `clear`; `/vg gaps` lists them; a Healthstone or a potion goes here), spells you choose (on the same card, or `/vg gaps spell add <spell>`: a link, an ID or a name; `spell remove <spell>`), and with "Add the manager's cooldowns" ticked (a reload applies it) the spells the game's manager tracks in those two rows (not the ones it hides until you add them; add such a spell as a chosen spell), each drawn as a Vanguard icon with its sweep (a spell with no running cooldown shows without a sweep; where the client hides a spell's cooldown and gives no timer for it, its icon is left out; charges, tracked buffs and buff bars stay with the game's manager, which is left as it is), your pet's abilities from the pet bar, and totems you have down (not for a shaman while the class bars show them). Each sweep takes the client's duration object where it gives one; where the client hides a cooldown's numbers the icon shows nothing rather than a guess. A source the client lacks adds nothing and raises no error. Nothing on it is clickable, and it works the same in combat. It has no fades or sounds.

## Cooldown Manager glow

Cooldown Manager glow (off until switched on: "Glow a buff near its end" on the Cooldowns page, with the share on the slider beside it, 30 % by default, 10 to 60; or `/vg cdmglow [on|off|<percent>]`, which says so when the module is switched off): a Cooldown Manager icon whose aura has less than that share of its time left gets the accent glow ring, for spells that lack the game's own pandemic flag (heal-over-time spells especially). It decorates the game's own icon and never copies it. Where the client can judge the aura's duration against a curve, that result goes straight to the glow, in combat too; otherwise the time is read out of combat only, and when the time is hidden, or the game hides which aura an icon tracks, the glow stays off with no error. The glow is static (no fade). Looked at four times a second.

## Cooldown Manager keys

Cooldown Manager keys (off until switched on: "Key text on the manager's icons" on the Cooldowns page, or `/vg cdmkeys [on|off]`; applied at once): each icon in the game's Cooldown Manager essential and utility rows gets the key you press for its spell as small text in its top-right corner. The key is read from Vanguard's own action bars: the first shown button that holds the spell (or a macro that casts it), with its binding in short form ("S1" for Shift-1). It decorates the game's own icon and copies nothing. A spell that is not on a Vanguard bar (Vanguard's bars off, or the game's own bars in use), or an icon whose spell the client will not name, shows no text and raises no error. It follows your bars and bindings as you change them, in combat too, and has no fades or sounds.
