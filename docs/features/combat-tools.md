# Combat tools

What Vanguard does here, in full. This page is part of the behaviour spec; the [README](../../README.md) has the overview and [docs/README.md](../README.md) lists every page.

## Boss mod docks

Two spots for boss mods' timer bars, Boss timers (the normal bars, right of center above the action bars) and Boss timers, soon (the big bars a timer moves to as it runs out, top center), shown as handles in unlock mode so you can move them with the rest of the layout. `/vg dock`, or the button under Combat tools → Boss mods (there while one is loaded), lines Deadly Boss Mods' bars up with them through DBM's own settings; for BigWigs, which has no such setting, chat says where the spots are so you can drag its anchors there. Nothing moves on its own, and nothing moves in combat.

## Fight history

Fight history (off until you turn it on under Combat tools): a sparkline of your DPS over the last 20 fights, with a tooltip, under the damage meter. Each fight is one bar, as tall as its share of your best, the latest in your accent color; a fight whose figure the game kept hidden leaves an empty slot. Hovering lists each fight, newest first, with its length, your DPS and, in a group, your place ("#1  1:23  412 dps  #2 of 5"); `/vg fights` prints the same. Fights are kept even while the strip is off, and forgotten when you log out. **Fight recap**: `/vg recap` shows the last fight's interrupts, dispels, damage taken and deaths from the game's damage meter in your chat (a part the meter has nothing for, or hides, is left out), and `/vg recap say|party|raid|instance|guild` sends that line to the channel; it is sent only when you type it and never in combat, when the meter is hidden. "Recap in chat after each fight" (off until you switch it on, under Combat tools) prints the line to your own chat when a fight is recorded. It has its own visibility (Always, On mouseover, ...) and moves with `/vg unlock`.

## Vanguard meter

Vanguard meter (off until you turn it on under Combat tools → Vanguard meter, or `/vg meter on`): a small flat, movable panel with the top lines of the game's damage meter for the current fight, up to ten (three to ten, eight by default), each a theme bar in the player's class color with a rank, a name and a per-second figure (yours in the accent). Click the title to step through the kinds (damage and healing per second, damage and healing done, damage taken, interrupts, dispels, deaths; `/vg meter <kind>` or the options pick one) and right-click it to step through the segments (this fight, overall, the previous fight; `/vg meter this|overall|previous`); the title says the segment when it isn't this fight's. Click a line to open a panel of that player's spells for the kind and segment shown (name, bar and total from the game's meter; click the panel's title, or the same line again, to close it; each line shows its share of the total after its figure ("12.3K  45%") when the game gives plain figures, which the **Show each line's share of the total** switch turns off; the game may keep it empty in combat; on the Deaths view a click also puts the game's death recap link for that player in your chat). `/vg meter report [say|party|raid|instance|guild] [lines]` puts the top lines (five by default, ten at most) in your chat or sends them to that channel, out of combat only and holding only plain names and figures; `/vg meter reset` clears the game's segments. The panel's width (200 to 600) is set under General → Layout (**Its width**) or in the unlock popover; its lines follow. The panel's title row has **Report** and **Reset** buttons, and Combat tools → Vanguard meter has the same (**Report the meter's top lines**, **Reset the game's damage meter**) and a **Report the meter to** pick (your chat, or say, party, raid, instance or guild, used by both Report buttons), and a **Meter shows** and **Meter segment** pick. It reads the game's meter every half second, in combat too: the figures are handed to the bars and texts as the game gives them and never counted, compared or sorted by Vanguard, so a line the client hides is drawn blank rather than guessed, and a list the client hides shows "No damage yet this fight" (or "No healing yet this fight" on the healing view). It starts at the bottom right, just left of the bags and menu bar and clear of the tooltip corner; the game's own meter is left alone (turn it off in the game's settings to see only this one). It has its own visibility (Always, On mouseover, ...) and moves with `/vg unlock`.

## Roll tracker

Roll tracker (on by default; switches under Combat tools): a small panel, movable with `/vg unlock`, listing the group's `/roll` results, highest first, with the top roll (or rolls, on a tie) in your accent color. Each result is read from the game's roll line in the system chat, in the client's own language; a line that doesn't match, or that the client hides, is skipped. A round is the rolls in one range (1-100, 1-6...) within 30 seconds of each other; the panel shows the latest round and goes 30 seconds after the last roll. Where the client keeps a loot history for the group's drops (Forever's encounter loot history), the panel also lists the last four items the group rolled for, with each member's choice (Need, Offspec, Transmog, Greed, No roll, Pass) and roll, the winner in the accent and "rolling" until the item has a winner or everyone passed; where it doesn't, or the history holds nothing for a roll, that part stays empty with no error and the `/roll` list still works. Nothing rolls for you. A name from another realm or ruleset keeps its "-Realm"; one from yours shows by name. Up to 40 rolls are kept this session. `/vg rolls` lists them, newest first, and `/vg rolls clear` forgets them.

## Officer loot tools

Officer loot tools (off until switched on: `/vg loot on`, or the switch under Combat tools → Officer loot tools): for a raid leader or officer, a soft-reserve list and a group roster. The list is kept on this character (not in the profile, not synced): `/vg loot add <player> <item>` adds a reserve (the item typed, or a link shift-clicked into the command), `/vg loot remove <player> <item>` takes one off, `/vg loot list` prints them by item and `/vg loot clear` forgets them; **Add a reserve** on the card asks for the same in a box. Next to the roll tracker, a roll by a player who reserved the item being rolled for is marked "[SR]" after their name, and a loot item the loot history lists shows a "Reserved:" line with who reserved it and "[SR]" by those players. The item a round of `/roll`s is for is the newest one the loot history says is still rolling; `/vg loot focus <item>` names one yourself (`/vg loot focus` alone goes back to following the loot history), since a `/roll` line says nothing about its item. Names are compared without case and without a "-Realm" ending. The **roster** (`/vg loot roster`, or **Show or hide the roster** on the card) is a small panel, movable with `/vg unlock`, listing the group, tanks then healers then damage then the rest, each by name: the name in the class color (gray when offline), then the role set in the game's group tools, "Lead" or "Asst", and "Offline" or "Dead"; the heading says how many are in the group and how many are offline. It redraws when the group, a role, a connection or a death changes, and only reads what the game shows plainly. Sharing is by hand and never syncs: addon messages are limited in instances, so nothing is sent between players' addons. **Put the list in the chat box for the party** (or the raid) opens the chat box with the list typed in behind `/p` or `/raid` when you click it; you read it and press Enter, and a long list comes in parts (click again for the next), an item with many reserves carried on as "Item (cont.): ..." so every part fits the chat box. **Print the reserve list to my chat** shows it to you alone. Nothing rolls, assigns or trades loot for anyone, and nothing is sent for you.
