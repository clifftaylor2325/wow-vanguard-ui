# Options and setup

What Vanguard does here, in full. This page is part of the behaviour spec; the [README](../../README.md) has the overview and [docs/README.md](../README.md) lists every page.

## Situations

Record a different layout and settings for raids, PvP, instances or resting; they apply out of combat as you move between them. One situation holds at a time, the first of PvP (a battleground or arena), Instance (a dungeon or raid instance), Raid, Party, Resting and Solo. To set one up, pick **Record for: Raid** on the Situations page (or `/vg situation record raid`), move frames with `/vg unlock` (spot, scale, opacity, links) and change settings as usual, then **Stop recording and save** (`/vg situation stop`; the button is greyed out while nothing is recording): only what you changed is kept. While you record, a banner along the top of the options window, and at the top of the screen while frames are unlocked, says "Editing as: Raid · changes apply only in Raid"; its **Stop and save** button ends the edit, saves what changed (chat says what was kept) and puts your own layout back. The banner also goes when the frames lock (by hand or for combat), on a profile change and at logout. An override can hold any frame's spot, scale and opacity, what it's anchored to, the sizes it matches and its own size, the info strip's, menu bar's and XP bars' visibility, the info strip's blocks, the nameplates' levels, threat colors, target border and quest marks, and the Calm HUD, and the Situations page lists them; nothing else is recorded. Any other setting, including every one that needs a reload, changes your own profile even while you record: while you record, the options dim most of those settings, and pointing at one says "Not recorded for Raid: this setting changes your base profile." Leaving the situation puts your own layout back, and your profile is always saved and exported with your own values. A change to something the active override sets lasts until you leave the situation. The page says which situation you're in and what its override changes; switch them all off there. Its **Your situations** card lists every situation with an override and what it changes, "(now)" on the one you're in, each with a **Clear** button (greyed while that situation is being recorded or the frames are unlocked); with none it says "No overrides yet. Pick Record for to make one." With the Situations module off under Modules, no override applies and the page says so. Profiles carry their overrides (an export string has them too, as overrides).

## Presets and options links for other addons

`/vg` → Profiles lists presets, profile strings you apply with one click (after a question, and a backup of your profile first): Vanguard's own Healer, Damage and Tank (raid frames, raid auras, raid healing and nameplates only; your layout and everything else stay; Healer and Damage are what setup's Healing and Damage and tanking layouts set) and any an addon offers with `Vanguard.RegisterPreset(name, string, info)`. `/vg preset <name>` does the same from chat or a macro, and `Vanguard.OpenOptions(page, setting)` lets an addon or macro open the options window on a page and row. Nothing is applied until you say yes, and not in combat. Addon authors: see [docs/API.md](../API.md).

## Options

`/vg`, the addons button, or the **Vanguard** button at the top of the game menu (Escape), in an opaque window (the world doesn't show through it, under a short page either) whose pages scroll with the mouse wheel or a thin thumb beside them. The window opens three quarters of the screen tall (at most the screen less 50, and never under 600), and fits again after a UI scale or screen size change. When Vanguard has caught errors this session, the addons button carries a small accent-coloured count (99+ at most) and the options window's footer says how many (click that line to open them in the copy window, as `/vg errors` does); with none, or after `/vg errors clear`, there is no badge. Pointing at the addons button shows Vanguard's version, what each click does (left-click: options; right-click: move frames, out of combat) and how many errors were caught this session. The game's AddOns list files Vanguard under a "UI Overhaul" heading where the client groups addons that way. Turn any module off under Modules to keep the default one. A setting changed from its default gets a small accent dot by its caption (point at it to see the default, such as "Default: On" or "Default: Follow the theme"; click it to put the default back), and a page holding any such setting gets one in the list on the left. A setting that applies only after a reload wears a small grey "reload" tag after its caption (point at the row for "Takes effect after /reload."). Typing in the search box lists the settings that match under it, each with the page it is on; click one, or press Enter for the first, to open its page with the row marked ("colour blind" finds Safer colours, "font size" finds Text size, and "changed" lists every setting that differs from its default). The search also finds buttons ("move frames" finds Move frames; a button you pick is only marked, never pressed), the names in a choice ("square" finds Corners), the small note under a setting, and "reload" finds the settings that apply after a reload. It lists eight at most; past that its last line says how many more there are ("12 more: type more words"). A page with settings that have defaults ends with a **This page** card whose **Reset this page** button asks, then puts every changed setting on that page back. The Modules page groups its switches under the same names as the list on the left, with a **Show** choice (All, On, Off). A module that is on there while its own switch inside is still off (the meter, mail tools, chat history and the others that start off) says so in small muted text beside its name, such as "On, but its own switch is off (Combat tools)", until you switch it on on that page. The list has a page for each area: the unit, raid and action bar pages, Quests, Nameplates, Situations and the rest, then **Bars and bags** (XP and reputation bars, bags and bank, the menu strip and the quest tracker), **Map** (minimap, waypoints, your buffs, the info strip, other addons' feeds), **Chat** (the chat window, chat watch, the group board), **Game windows** (the game's windows, auto-accept, tooltips), **Cooldowns** (the game's Cooldown Manager, Vanguard's gaps row and the buff glow, with a setup window) and **Combat tools** (the game's damage meter and alerts, the meter, boss mods, fight history, the roll tracker) and **Town and gear** (the auction house, merchants and errands, the craft queue, mail tools, spell ranks, Legacy, instance prep, gear upgrades). These pages were one page, Bars, map, chat, before; with many pages the list's entries get a little shorter so they all fit. With the window open, Up and Down go through the pages and Tab (Shift-Tab back) through a page's settings, an accent ring marking the one Tab is on, Enter or Space pressing it; other keys still reach the game, and nothing is taken while you type in the search or a box, or in a fight. The top of General shows a unit frame (with your character's name) and an action button (with Vanguard's crest) in your accent, font, text size and bar texture as you change them. Most changes apply at once; the rest show a Reload now button. WoW Forever lets an addon reload only from a click on a secure button, so that's what Vanguard's Reload buttons are; one built during a fight becomes one when the fight ends, and until then it asks you to type `/reload`.

## Other addons

At login Vanguard notices addons that do a job one of its modules also does (bag addons such as Bagnon, AdiBags, ArkInventory, BetterBags and Baganator; nameplate addons such as Plater; EllesmereUI's unit frame, action bar, nameplate and raid frame parts; action bar (ElvUI and Tukui count here), unit frame, raid frame, click-casting, cast bar, minimap, chat and tooltip addons; Auctionator and TradeSkillMaster for the Auction helper) and says so once in chat. The Modules page of `/vg` then shows an **Other addons** card with **Turn off** (Vanguard's module goes off, with a reload offered) or **Keep both** for each; nothing changes until you click, and an answer isn't asked again in that profile.

## First-run setup

The first time you log in on an account, a four-step wizard asks for a layout (Damage, Tanking, Healing, Versus deck, Like the game's, or Keep my current layout, which moves nothing; the layout for your role, by specialization or else by group role, is marked "your role": Healing for a healer, Tanking for a tank, Damage for anyone else), sizes for your screen, an accent (color swatches), a raid frame style (three small raid tiles, each drawn in its style in your class color) the Calm HUD switch (off unless you turn it on) and two readability switches, **Larger text (115%)** (Text size 115%) and **Colour vision: Safer colours** (the General page's Safer colours switch; its colors settle at the reload Done offers), both off unless you turn them on, then unlocks your frames so you can move them (its page says that a click on a handle and the arrow keys nudge it, that a right-click gives a frame's anchor, scale and opacity, and that the bar at the top saves and locks, or exits without saving) (the same unlock mode as `/vg unlock`, or **Move frames** under General in `/vg`; `/vg lock` or Done locks them again). It opens on Keep my current layout and Keep my current sizes, the first time too, so Vanguard starts from where you are: Next, Next, Next, Done changes nothing. The screen step offers 1080p, 1440p, 4K and ultrawide presets and Pixel-perfect, and marks the one for your screen "your screen": each sets the game's UI scale (1080p 80%, 1440p and ultrawide 70%, 4K 65%, the bottom of the game's own range; it sizes the unit frames, which every layout draws in UI units), the action button size (38 px, the default), the raid tile size (100 x 48, or 110 x 48 on the bigger screens; it shows after a reload, which setup offers) and the text size (100% to 110%). **Pixel-perfect (sharpest)** instead leaves the game's UI scale alone and turns on Vanguard's pixel-perfect scale, so every line lands on whole screen pixels: one UI unit is one screen pixel, or two on a screen about 1600 pixels tall or more (4K), and it grows your screen preset's action buttons and text to make up for the smaller scale (raid tiles keep the preset's size). The UI scale is the same setting as the game's Options → Graphics → UI Scale, applied out of combat; the other presets turn Vanguard's pixel-perfect scale off, since that would replace it. The step says which sets your UI scale now (the game's slider, or Vanguard's pixel-perfect scale while it's on), and that a preset moves the game's slider. Before setup first changes anything in a run (a layout or screen preset applied, an accent or raid style picked), it copies the profile in use to its backup profile ("<name> backup", the same one imports use) and says so in chat. Done always ends with a reload offer (after the export question on a first run), so everything setup set shows as it will from now on. It waits until you're out of combat. Done finishes it, and so does **Skip setup** on its first step (which applies nothing); the close button and Escape only close it, and it opens again at your next login until it's finished. Each new character on an account that has finished setup gets it too, with a Profile step first: share the profile your other characters use (the one used last, at the last logout of any of them; the character starts on it; changes there change them too), or give this character a profile of its own, named after it, that starts as a copy of that one, with no reload; Back doesn't return to the Profile step once a profile of its own is made, and setup opened again (`/vg setup`, or the next login after closing it early) doesn't ask again. Characters that played before this came in aren't asked. To run it again: `/vg setup`, or **Run setup** under General → Layout in `/vg`; run again (or on a new character sharing a profile), it starts on Keep my current layout and Keep my current sizes, so clicking through changes only what you pick. **Start over** on the Profiles page asks first (saying every setting in the profile goes back to the defaults, for every character using it), copies the profile to its backup profile, resets it and opens setup after the reload. **Reset profile** asks and backs up the same way, and the profile list's **Delete** and **Copy here** say in a tooltip what they lose and ask "Sure?" on the first click (leaving the page or closing the window takes the question back, so a later single click only asks again). Damage is Vanguard's defaults (picking it puts back anything another layout moved or sized) with a taller target frame (40 instead of 36, growing up from its bottom edge) and the Damage preset's settings (no power bars, role sort, HoTs, incoming heals or buff watch on the raid frames, two raid debuffs, six debuffs with timers and threat colours on nameplates, and no buffs on players' nameplates); Tanking is the same with the tallest target frame (44) and the Tank preset's settings (role sort and the aggro strip on the raid frames, threat colours, the target border and three buffs on nameplates); Versus deck is your role's centered layout (with its settings) with the Versus deck turned on and your frame and your target's at one scale and height, and any other layout turns it off; Like the game's puts your player and target frames along the top left as the game has them (the pet under the player, the target of target and focus beside the target), the party and raid down the left side, the boss frames on the right under your buffs, and the raid tools and main tanks near the top center; Healing turns click casting on, puts the party and raid frames back at their default spots (over your player frame, a raid of 25 or 40 stacked at the left of your character; see Centered layout) drawn larger (115%), draws your player and target frames smaller (90%) and sets the Healer preset's settings (power bars and role sort on the raid frames, three raid debuffs, HoTs, incoming heals, shields and the buff watch, two debuffs, no threat colours and no buffs on players' nameplates; the frames on screen change at once, the aura rows after the reload Done offers). Healing doesn't change how the groups are laid out; that's **Groups as** on the Raid frames page in `/vg` (Columns, the default, or Rows; a change takes a reload). A test checks all three layouts with groups as columns and as rows, solo and with 5, 25 and 40 players (raids above 10 are drawn smaller), on a 1365 x 768 screen at UI scale 1, on 1920 x 1080 pixel-perfect, with each screen preset on the screen it's made for, and with Pixel-perfect at 1080p, 1440p, 4K and ultrawide: with Vanguard's default spots nothing overlaps and nothing leaves the screen. To put the raid frames somewhere else, drag them with `/vg unlock` (or **Move frames** under General in `/vg`).

## Settings guard

WoW Forever's beta has been reported to lose addon settings between sessions, so at each logout Vanguard leaves the same stamp in the account's settings file and in the character's own file, with a backup of the character's profile (as a profile string) beside the character's stamp. If at the next login the account file comes back blank, or older than the character's last session (each character is judged by its own stamp, so every character is asked after a loss, not only the first to log in), chat says the saved settings didn't load, a question offers to restore the profile from the character's backup (or, without one, to paste a profile string you exported), and the General and Profiles pages in `/vg` start with the same notice, with **Restore the backup**, **Paste a profile string** and **Start fresh: run setup**; setup waits meanwhile. A restore offers the reload that applies it. Until you answer (or import or pick a profile), logout leaves the stamps and the backup as they were, so the next login offers it again. While the profile a backup names is missing (after a pasted string or a fresh start), logout keeps that backup rather than replacing it. If the character's own file comes back blank while the account file remembers the character, the character goes back to the profile it used (after a reload, which is offered) and setup isn't asked again. A first run, or files saved before this came in, have no stamps, so none of this fires. Finishing setup for the first time (Done, not Skip) asks whether to export a profile string to keep outside the game. Whether an addon can stop the game overwriting its settings at logout is unknown, so Vanguard only notices and restores. The game also refuses an addon's saved file past a size it doesn't publish, so about 15 seconds after login (out of combat) Vanguard estimates the size of the account file (the text the game writes, walked once); over a 6 MB budget it drops the auction prices of the realm-and-side homes with the oldest prices, never the one you play on, and says how many in chat. If the game sends its own too-large signal (`SAVED_VARIABLES_TOO_LARGE`) Vanguard trims to half that budget the same way and says so; a file the game then returns blank is caught by the guard above. `/vg mem` first prints the estimate (account file against the budget, its three largest keys, and this character's file; one walk, out of combat).

## What's new

The first login after an update shows a short **What's new in Vanguard x.y** window: what changed in this version, grouped by module under small grey capitals headings, with "(off by default)" after a new thing that starts off. A line in the accent colour has a setting: click it and the options window opens on its page with that setting marked (the hint under the title, "Click a line to open its setting.", shows only when a line has one). It shows once per version for the whole account, out of combat, and never on top of setup: a new account goes through setup and skips it, and a new character sees it once its setup ends. `/vg whatsnew`, or **What's new** under General → Layout in `/vg`, opens it again.
