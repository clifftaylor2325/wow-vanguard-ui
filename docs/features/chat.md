# Chat

What Vanguard does here, in full. This page is part of the behaviour spec; the [README](../../README.md) has the overview and [docs/README.md](../README.md) lists every page.

## Chat window

The main chat window starts 200 tall and as wide as the room left of your player frame allows, up to 524 (416 on a 1080p screen at setup's 80 % scale, 524 at 1920 units), in the bottom-left, 40 units in so its side buttons have their column left of it; on a screen under 1600 units wide it tucks under the player frame at 372 x 144 (a window you already sized or moved keeps its size and spot, except one still at an earlier version's first size (420 x 140, 500 x 154 or 420 x 154), which is set to the new first size once; **Chat window size** under Chat sets Default, Larger or Largest, one and a half or two times that). The window, its scroll bar and the tab bar above it sit in one crisp rounded panel (four-pixel arcs, whatever the Corners setting) whose fill is the chat background, so chat reads over bright scenery; a one-pixel rule parts the bar from the text, and the panel goes with the **Chat background** switch (with Soft shadows under frames on, under General, the panel gets a small soft shadow like the HUD's frames). With Chat background off, a soft band in the panel colour at the Light strength takes the panel's place behind the text, the tab bar and the scroll column: clear at the left edge, solid behind the lines and fading out over the right 96 units, so the text still reads without a box; the tab bar and its tiles stay. **Chat background strength** under Chat → Chat (Light, Medium or Dark) sets how dark. The tabs are 20 tall, rounded and centred in the bar (as wide as their names, in the plain text colour): the shown tab is the one with a fill, raised with a thin lit line along its top, and the others are just their names on the bar; the tab row ends before the **Copy** and **Find** tiles at the bar's right end (20 x 20 glyph tiles like the side buttons', two sheets and a magnifier, Copy at the end and Find left of it, each named by its tooltip), so spare tabs go to the game's overflow menu and never under the buttons, and a window you drag out of the dock gets its tab on its own bar. The Combat Log's filter buttons sit in a band of their own under the bar's rule, a darker strip two units in from the panel's edges (as the tab row sits in the bar) with no border of its own (the game keeps that band between the log's tab row and its text, docked or floating), so the log's panel, bar and tab row line up with the General tab's. The tabs draw over the panel, and keep the look when the game lays them out again (a click, a new window, a resize). The scroll bar on the right is a slim thumb inside the panel with no boxed arrows, and the scroll-to-bottom tile under it (shown when you scroll up) takes a thin accent edge while the game flashes it for new lines below. The typing box sits under the panel, a gap below it and as tall as the tab bar, the panel's full width, with a two-pixel rail down its left edge in the colour of the channel you are typing to (Say, Guild, a numbered channel: the game's own chat colours) and your text in the chat font; it rests at the game's 35 % and comes to full over 0.2 s as you start typing, dropping back over 0.05 s after. Chat lines carry the same drop shadow as every other Vanguard text (the Outline switch under General still applies) with two units of air between lines, so they read at Light too. **Chat text fades** (under Chat) keeps each line for 60 seconds and then fades it out over 0.6 s (Never keeps every line; After 10 seconds is the quieter choice; in a fight the fade takes 0.15 s, and with Reduce motion the line goes at once). **Keep chat tabs visible** (on by default; under Chat) stops the game fading the tabs to faint until the mouse comes near; unticked, they fade as the game has them, but never fainter than 60 % (the game's 20 % is too faint on a bar you can see), and the mouse brings them to full. **Hide the tab bar until the mouse is over the chat** (off by default; under Chat) is the glass look: the tab bar with its rule and tiles fades out 0.6 s after the mouse has been away from the chat for 4 seconds and comes back in 0.1 s when it returns (0.15 s at most in a fight, at once with Reduce motion), stays while a tab is flashing for new lines, and the tabs themselves show only under the mouse or while they alert, at the game's own pace. The **Pixel-perfect scale** switch (General → Look) picks the whole multiple for your screen, as setup's Pixel-perfect choice does (one unit is two screen pixels from 4K up), so the UI and chat are not tiny on a big screen.

## Chat extras

Web addresses in chat become links in your accent color; click one and it opens selected, ready to copy. The **Copy** tile (two sheets) at the right end of the chat's tab bar (or `/vg copy`) opens the recent chat as plain text to copy. Short channel names (off until you switch it on): `[2. Trade - City]` shows as `[T]`, `[Guild]` as `[G]`, `[Raid Leader]` as `[RL]`, and clicking the short tag still opens the channel. Ctrl + mouse wheel jumps to the top or the bottom of a chat window. The game's own chat timestamps and class-colored names can be set under `/vg` → Chat too (they are the game's settings, so the game's options show the same). Chat the game hides from addons (in instances) is left untouched, never shortened, and out of the copy.

## Chat watch

Phrases that ring a sound and show a toast you can click to whisper the sender. Add phrases ("LFM", "WTS Devilsaur") under Chat → Chat watch or with `/vg watch add LFM`, and tick the channels to watch (numbered channels such as Trade and LFG, and yell, by default; say, guild, party and raid, whispers if you like). When a line says one (any case, item links included), the whisper sound plays and a small accent-edged toast at the top of the screen shows the sender (without the realm when it's yours; a click still whispers the full name) and the line for 8 seconds; click it to whisper them, right-click to dismiss. When the line links an item you have an auction price for (from your scans and searches on this realm and faction), the toast shows that price beside the line, "AH 12s 50c" (the first linked item that has one; the line itself is never changed, and a switch under Chat watch turns it off). One alert per sender and phrase every 30 seconds, and your own lines (your name, alone or with your realm) never ring. Chat itself is untouched. In dungeons, raids and encounters, where the game hides chat from addons, nothing matches. Up to 20 phrases of 40 characters; the toast moves with `/vg unlock`.

## Chat filter

Off until you switch it on (`/vg` → Chat, or `/vg filter on`). Lines in numbered channels such as Trade and General, say and yell that sell gold, sell boosts or recruit for a guild (each its own switch), hold a word you added (`/vg filter add <word>`, up to 30 of 40 characters), or repeat what the same sender said in the last minute (a line of 12 characters or more; "inv" never counts), go to a chat tab called Filtered instead of the chat (or are hidden, with `/vg filter hide`); the tab's name shows how many were caught, "Filtered (12)". Each line there starts with why it was caught, and clicking the sender's name lets their lines through again until you log out. The tab is made the first time it's needed, out of combat, and found again by its name at later logins. If the client can't make it, nothing is filtered. Whispers, guild, party and raid lines, and your own, are never filtered. Where the game hides chat from addons (dungeons, raids, encounters) a line is not read and passes through. The categories are short word lists in the addon, not a list of names, so some spam will get through and the odd honest line may be caught: the Filtered tab is there so you can see.

## Chat history

Off until you switch it on (`/vg` → Chat, or `/vg history on`). The lines in your chat windows come back after a `/reload` or the next login, each in its own tab and in the colour it had, between two grey dash rules, "—— Chat from your last session (N lines, until Mon 14:32) ——" above and "—— End of the earlier chat ——" below. Each window keeps at most 100, 200 or 500 lines (200 to start) no older than 1 day, 3 days, a week or a month (3 to start); older and surplus lines are dropped as you play and at logout, so the saved file stays small (500 lines in all ten windows is about half a megabyte at most). The lines are saved for this character only, in its own file, never in a profile or a profile export. Whispers are not kept unless you tick Keep whispers (`/vg history whispers`); a whisper is recognised by the game showing one, and by its sender link. Windows that open for a whisper are never kept. `/vg history clear` (or the Clear button) forgets everything saved, and `/vg history` says what is kept. Where the game hides chat from addons (dungeons, raids, encounters) a line is not read and is not kept. The combat log and the Filtered tab are never kept. The lines come back once you are in the world, and the game's own timestamps (if on) show the time they came back. A window keeps the game's own line limit, so a long history may show fewer lines than were saved.

## Chat windows

Under `/vg` → Chat, each chat window that is showing has a Width and a Height slider (set instead of dragging its corner, saved in the game's own chat layout), Lock chat windows is the game's lock for all of them, and Move floating windows with /vg unlock (reload) makes each window you pulled out of the dock a handle like the main chat window's. Sliders for a window in a hidden tab appear once its tab is showing and the page is reopened.

## Chat tabs

Under `/vg` → Chat → Chat tabs, pick a tab from the Tab choice, then Rename this tab or Close this tab (the main tab can't be closed), make one with New chat tab (names are at most 16 characters), and switch what it shows with Show Say, Yell, Guild, Party, Raid, Whispers, Loot and System messages. Tabs are made and closed out of combat. Reordering tabs is still done by dragging them in the game. Chat tabs wear Vanguard's tab look (20 tall and rounded in the tab bar, the shown tab raised, the others sunk, a hover glow) with the game's alert glow on a tab that has new lines in the accent colour; the game still flashes it.

## Chat search

The Find tile (a magnifier) in the main chat window's tab bar (left of Copy; Find button in the chat's tab bar under `/vg` → Chat turns it off at once) and Find in chat on the Chat page open the find box: type a word in the field at its top and press Enter or Find, and the matching lines are listed under it as plain text with each match in your accent colour, newest 200: the lines in your windows plus, when Chat history is on, saved lines that no window shows any more. Case does not matter; another word in the same box searches again, and the box keeps the last word and lines until the next search. Lines the game hides from addons are never read.

## Chat bubbles

Speech bubbles over heads in the world sit on a flat, slightly see-through slate panel (the chat's own material: no sheen, no shadow) with their text in Vanguard's font, instead of the game's rounded white bubble; **Text-only bubbles** (off) drops the panel too, so the text floats over the speaker; inside instances the game keeps its own bubbles (the engine does not let addons touch them there); `/vg` → Modules → Chat bubbles turns it off (reload).

## Group board

Group board (`/vg lfg`, or **Open the group board** under Chat → Group board): WoW Forever has no Dungeon Finder, so groups form in chat. Vanguard reads the lines posted in the numbered channels (General, Trade, LookingForGroup) and yell (say and guild too if you tick them) and lists each player's latest looking-for-more or looking-for-group post on a board, newest first: LFM in your accent or LFG in blue, the dungeon or raid with its level range in its difficulty color (vanilla's dungeons by name or short name: "SFK", "BRD", "DM east"; "Any dungeon" when the post names none), the roles it names (tank, healer, DPS), who posted it and how long ago. Hover a row for the whole line. A filter button shows all posts, only looking for more or only looking for a group, and **Only dungeons for my level** (on by default) hides posts for dungeons whose range starts more than two levels above you or ends below you. Posts go after 10 minutes (5 or 20 if you like), and a newer post from the same player replaces the older one. Click a row and the chat box opens a whisper to them with a message filled in: to a group looking for more, "Hi! Level 24 Mage (Healer) for Shadowfang Keep, if you still need one." (your role is set under Group board), to a player looking for a group, "Hi! We're forming Shadowfang Keep, want to join?". Nothing is ever sent for you: you read it, change it if you like, and press Enter. In dungeons and raids, where the game hides chat from addons, nothing is read. When the board is empty, the line under it says how many chat lines it has heard since login and what became of them, and `/vg lfg probe` adds the channels you are in and the board's switches, so you can tell a quiet chat from a board that hears nothing.
