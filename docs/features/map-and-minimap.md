# Map, minimap, bags and more

What Vanguard does here, in full. This page is part of the behaviour spec; the [README](../../README.md) has the overview and [docs/README.md](../README.md) lists every page.

## Everything else

XP and reputation bars (their numbers sit centred on a bar at least as tall as the text, and on the thin default bars at the left end for XP and the right end for reputation, so with Numbers set to always the two lines never overlap; hover the XP bar for what is left in the level, how far through, rested XP, about how many kills to level at your last kill's XP, and the XP waiting in quests ready to turn in; `/vg` → Modules → XP and reputation turns the tooltip off), square minimap (the zone name in bold Fira Sans, which the faction look never swaps for its own font, on a see-through band along its top edge and the coordinates on one along its bottom, each band with a thin accent rule on its inner edge, so both read over bright ground; hover the zone name for the zone, its subzone and whose territory it is (the tooltip ends with "Click: world map" and "Wheel: zoom"), click it to open or close the world map (in a fight chat says the map waits, since the game opens none of its windows for an addon then); brass diamonds on its corners, in your accent), chat skin (the chat frame's side buttons, from the chat menu to each window's minimize box and scroll-to-bottom arrow, lose their round art and become window controls (the control tile shows under the mouse): a theme glyph on each, or the button's own icon where that icon shows a state, as on the voice and text-to-speech buttons; the main window's stack down from its tab bar's top, the chat menu first, then Quick Join, voice and text to speech, a hidden one leaving no gap), combined bag (switched on once, not at every login, and given back when you switch the module off; a profile you import switches it on once on your computer too; with a Sort button beside the search box where the game can sort bags, replacing the game's own (the search box narrowed so Sort fits inside the window; without a gamepad the game's leftover gamepad backpack icon in the top-right corner, which covered Sort, is hidden, as the backpack is on the bag bar), item slots bordered in each item's quality color, search dimming kept, and the game's search box in the theme; its slots sorted into sections: quest items, equipment, consumables, trade goods, other, junk (gray items) and empty slots, each under a header in small grey capitals (the options cards' section header) and side by side where they fit, with a clear gap above each header, the slots, headers, search row and money line all 12 px inside the window's edges, and the window growing to fit them (the game draws a bag too tall for your screen a little smaller, as it does its own); an item the game hasn't loaded yet waits under Other and moves when it has; in combat the bag keeps its layout and catches up when combat ends (opened in combat, it shows the game's plain grid until then); one switch under Bars and bags gives back the plain grid at once; your own groups get their own header above the built-in ones: the **Groups** button on the search row makes one, files the item on your cursor in it, sends it to the open bank, moves it first or deletes it, and **Filter** types a ready-made search (rare or better, bind on equip, armor, a group of yours); `/vg bag add <name> <item>` and the other `/vg bag` words do the same; the first group in the list wins when an item is in two; the bag's search box also takes `ilvl>20`, `q:epic`, `type:armor`, `bind:boe` and `cat:<name>` words, any number together with plain words) and the bank in the same look, and themed tooltips with class colors and a Target line.

## Strip under the minimap

Two columns of plain figures, one pair a row, so nothing runs together. Durability ("100% durability"; orange at 25% or less, red at 10% or less) beside your free bag slots (a soul bag's or quiver's left out, as on the backpack); gold on its own row; fps and latency ("27 fps · 44 ms") beside ammo (for any class whose ranged weapon needs it, where the game says so, else hunters, warriors and rogues; red at none) (soul shards for warlocks, counted across your bags and the reagent bag, red at none); and the time followed by the day and date ("21:04  Tue 13 Oct") with a calendar button at its right end that opens the calendar like `/calendar` (in a fight chat says the calendar waits instead; the game's own calendar button stays put away). While you level, a fifth row adds your XP per hour this session beside the time to your next level at that pace, and a sixth your gold per hour (red while you're spending); they wait a minute for the numbers to settle, and the XP ones hide at the level cap. Beside the gold rate it can show what your auction sales came to this session, "AH +12g 5s" (from the sales ledger; off until you turn it on). A seventh row can show your DPS in the last fight, from the game's damage meter (off until you turn it on; it updates when a fight ends, and stays hidden where the game keeps it secret), and, in a group, your place in that fight's DPS ("#2 of 5", the place the game's own meter shows; also off until you turn it on). Each part can be turned off; one the game can't answer stays hidden. The strip is always shown unless you pick another visibility for it (On mouseover, In combat, Hidden or the action bars' row of conditions); so are the XP and reputation bars, which share one setting. The quest tracker sits just under the strip with room for all seven rows.

## Broker strip

Other addons' data feeds (LibDataBroker data sources, the kind info-bar addons show: gold trackers, durability, guild counts and so on) in a thin strip along the top edge of the screen, left of center, movable with `/vg unlock`. Pick the feeds under Map → Data from other addons (up to four, in the order picked); each shows its icon and text, its own tooltip on hover, and takes your clicks. Launchers stay in the minimap drawer. Vanguard doesn't bundle LibDataBroker: the addons that share feeds load it, so with none of them the strip stays hidden.

## Minimap drawer

Other addons' round minimap buttons are gathered into a drawer beside the map; the small "..." button on the map's left edge opens it (it only shows once there's a button to gather). A button an addon makes later, when you turn it on in that addon's settings or it loads on demand, joins the drawer at once (after the fight, in one); a protected button is left on the map. It needs Vanguard's Minimap module: with that off (or turned off for SexyMap), the buttons stay on the game's map, and its row under Modules says so.

## Waypoints

`/way 45.2 61.8` puts a waypoint on the map you're on and the game's own navigation arrow points to it; `/way Elwynn Forest 42 65 Inn` names a zone (any case, or a unique start of its name) and a title, `/way #1429 42 65` a map by its id, and `/way clear` removes it. Commas work too (`45.2, 61.8`). `/way` is Vanguard's only while no other addon (TomTom, say) has it; `/vg way` always works. While the world map is open, a line along its bottom shows your coordinates and the cursor's. With OpenMap (an optional companion addon that draws one seamless map) loaded, the line shows only yours, since OpenMap's map isn't the zone the cursor would be read against, and the Map page says OpenMap is drawing the map. The floating pin and distance the game draws over the world for a waypoint or a supertracked quest take Vanguard's pin in your accent and the theme font (a party member's portrait and the corpse's tombstone stay the game's); the pin's place, distance and fading stay the game's own. All three are on by default, under Map. Ctrl-click on the world map puts a waypoint down (the game's own); the game leaves its arrow off for it, so Vanguard turns the arrow on (switch under Map → Waypoints, on by default). The last eight waypoints are kept: the Map page lists them as "Go to ..." buttons, `/way recent` lists them and `/way recent <n>` goes back to one. A Clear waypoint button sits on the world map while there is a waypoint, and on the Map page.
