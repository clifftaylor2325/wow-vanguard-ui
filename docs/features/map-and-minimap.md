# Map, minimap, bags and more

What Vanguard does here, in full. This page is part of the behaviour spec; the [README](../../README.md) has the overview and [docs/README.md](../README.md) lists every page.

## Everything else

XP and reputation bars (their numbers sit centred on a bar at least as tall as the text; on the thin default bars the XP numbers (and honor, which shares the XP bar) stay centred on the bar and the reputation numbers sit centred just above their bar, so with Numbers set to always the two lines never overlap (each is held to its bar's width, on one line, and ends in an ellipsis when it runs longer, as at a large Text size); hover the XP bar for what is left in the level, how far through, rested XP, about how many kills to level at your last kill's XP, and the XP waiting in quests ready to turn in; the reputation bar's numbers start with your standing ("Friendly"), and at the top standing it is a full bar reading just the faction and "Exalted"; hover the reputation bar for your standing, what the next one needs and how far through, and click it for the game's reputation list (not in a fight); `/vg` → Modules → XP and reputation turns both tooltips off; the XP bar hides at the level cap, while XP gain is locked and when the realm's rules switch the game's XP bar off, as the game's does, and is back the moment it is unlocked; the reputation bar hides when no faction is watched; at the level cap, or with the XP bar turned off, the reputation bar moves down into the XP bar's spot, unless you placed it yourself; on Retail, at the level cap the XP bar shows your honor level's progress in orange whenever the game's own bar would (honor watched as experience, or in a battleground), hover it for what the next honor level needs, and a watched major faction shows its renown, "Renown 12" in place of the standing, in blue as the game draws it, with what the next renown level needs, full at the top level), square minimap (the zone name in bold Fira Sans, which the faction look never swaps for its own font, on a see-through band along its top edge and the coordinates on one along its bottom (the game's own minimap coordinates line is hidden, and on Forever so is its day/night ring beside the old map spot; where the game draws its hybrid map, for places with no ground tiles, it shows over the panel under the map's markers), and the game's Toggle Minimap key (or the new-player tutorial) hides and shows the whole box with the map, each band with a thin accent rule on its inner edge, so both read over bright ground; hover the zone name for the zone, its subzone and whose territory it is (the tooltip ends with "Click: world map" and "Wheel: zoom"), click it to open or close the world map (in a fight chat says the map waits, since the game opens none of its windows for an addon then); brass diamonds on its corners, in your accent), chat skin (the chat frame's side buttons, from the chat menu to each window's minimize box and scroll-to-bottom arrow, lose their round art and become window controls (the control tile shows under the mouse): a theme glyph on each, or the button's own icon where that icon shows a state, as on the voice and text-to-speech buttons; the main window's stack down from its tab bar's top, the chat menu first, then Quick Join, voice and text to speech, a hidden one leaving no gap), combined bag (switched on once, not at every login, and given back when you switch the module off (the value given back is this computer's own, whichever profile is on); a profile you import switches it on once on your computer too; with a Sort button beside the search box where the game can sort bags, replacing the game's own on the combined bag (with separate bag windows the backpack keeps the game's sort button; the search box narrowed so Sort fits inside the window, Sort itself widening to its whole label in a translation that runs longer, and Filter, Groups and Sort fitting their labels again when you change the Text size with the bag open; without a gamepad the game's leftover gamepad backpack icon in the top-right corner, which covered Sort, is hidden, as the backpack is on the bag bar), item slots bordered in each item's quality color, search dimming kept, and the game's search box in the theme; its slots sorted into sections: quest items, equipment, consumables, trade goods, other, junk (gray items; its header shows what your gray items sell for, those filed in a group of yours too, "Junk · 12s 40c", when the header has room for it) and empty slots (the backpack's slots the game keeps locked until you add an authenticator come last among them, as in the game's own bag), each under a header in small grey capitals (the options cards' section header; a section is at least two slots wide, and wider where its translated header needs it to show whole) and side by side where they fit, with a clear gap above each header, the slots, headers, search row and money line all 12 px inside the window's edges, and the window growing to fit them (the game draws a bag too tall for your screen a little smaller, as it does its own, down to its smallest size; the sectioned bag is also kept on the screen, so on a short screen a very full bag slides down instead of losing its title and Sort button past the top); an item the game hasn't loaded yet waits under Other and moves when it has; in combat the bag keeps its layout and catches up when combat ends (opened in combat, it shows the game's plain grid until then); one switch under Bars and bags gives back the plain grid at once; your own groups get their own header above the built-in ones: the **Groups** button on the search row makes one, files the item on your cursor in it (and takes it out again: Take held item out of its group), sends it to the open bank (every bag including the reagent bag, to the bank tab that is open; a stack the game would warn about, a refundable item bound for the Warband bank, stays in your bags and chat says so; once the game has moved them chat says how many stacks went and how many found no room (a stack still on its way after 5 seconds counts as gone)), moves it first or deletes it, and **Filter** types a ready-made search (rare or better, bind on equip, armor, a group of yours); `/vg bag add <name> <item>` and the other `/vg bag` words do the same; the first group in the list wins when an item is in two, and two saved groups whose names differ only in capitals show as one section, the first group's; the bag's search box, and the bank's, also takes `ilvl>20`, `q:epic`, `type:armor`, `bind:boe` (an item already bound to you counts as `bind:bop`) and `cat:<name>` words (both boxes hold 64 letters, where the game's hold 15, so a long group's search from Filter fits whole; `type:` takes the English class word, such as armor or weapon, on any client, or the client's own word for the type; on Retail `ilvl` and `q` compare the slot's own item level and quality, upgrades and bonus IDs included, not the item ID's base), any number together with plain words, and the answer holds as items move, the game refills a slot or you turn a bank page; the bag bar's buttons light by the same words, a button lit when its bag holds a match; on Retail the bank's side tabs dim by them too, a tab lit when any of its slots matches) and the bank in the same look, and themed tooltips with class colors and a Target line (the Links window and the floating pet tooltip with the theme's close cross, the comparison tooltips' "Currently Equipped" label on a flat panel, the battle pet tooltips themed, and no decorative edge art over the panel). A player's tooltip also says the guild and rank ("Guild: Night Watch (Officer)", or only "Guild rank: Officer" where the game's own tooltip already names the guild), the realm of a player from another realm ("Realm: Emberfall", unless the game's tooltip already carries it) and the role the group gave them ("Role: Tank", Healer or DPS), and your own tooltip adds your equipped item level, rounded down as the character pane shows it ("Item level 62"; other players get no item level, since the game only gives one after an inspect request), each with its own switch under Game windows → Tooltips (**Guild and rank**, **Realm, for players of another realm**, **Group role** and **Your own item level**, all on by default; a change applies at once), and none of them when the game hides the value. A tooltip the game gives its default spot sits in a movable corner (`/vg unlock` shows its handle, **Tooltip**), follows the cursor, or stays where the game puts it, chosen apart for three kinds under Game windows → Tooltips: **Unit tooltips in the world** (a player or mob under the mouse), **Unit tooltips on frames** (unit, party and raid frames) and **Tooltip position** for everything else (a spell on a bar, a mailbox); each starts in the corner, a profile that had one position keeps it for all three, and a change applies to the next tooltip. Tooltips the game anchors to what they describe (a bag item's) stay there. One switch, **IDs on item, spell and aura tooltips** (off by default; under More settings on the same card, and it applies at once), adds "Item ID 2589" to an item's tooltip and "Spell ID 133" to a spell's and to a buff's or debuff's (an aura's ID is its spell's); a profile that had the earlier Item IDs switch on keeps it on. On WoW Retail the bank's Bank and Warband tabs along its foot take the game windows' flat tabs (with the tabs switch under Game windows), the bank tabs down its right side and the tab for buying one become icon tiles that keep their picture and their selected glow, and the warband bank's gold row (Withdraw, Deposit and the money in a flat box) and deposit row (its deposit button and the Include reagents check box) take the theme; a warband tab's slots take the slot look and the search words as a character tab's do. On WoW Forever the bank's page tabs down its right side take the game windows' side-tab look (with the tabs switch under Game windows), as the character pane's do, its row of bank bag slots takes the item-slot look, and the Bag Slots and Costs lines, the money and the Purchase button (shown while a bag slot is left to buy) take the theme.

## Strip under the minimap

Two columns of plain figures, one pair a row, so nothing runs together. The strip (and the quest tracker under it) sits 12 units in from the right edge, just under the minimap at its usual size; with a round minimap (the Classic look, or Minimap shape set to Round) it sits 20 units lower, under the round map's coordinates band, and a square map keeps the higher spot. A spot you dragged them to stays. Durability ("100% durability"; orange at 25% or less, red at 10% or less) beside your free bag slots (a soul bag's or quiver's left out, as on the backpack; amber at 4 or fewer, red at none); gold on its own row; fps and latency ("27 fps · 44 ms") beside ammo (for any class whose ranged weapon needs it, where the game says so, else hunters, warriors and rogues; red at none) (soul shards for warlocks, counted across your bags and the reagent bag, red at none); the ammo and soul shard blocks are WoW Forever's, and Retail shows neither; and the time as the game's own clock shows it (realm or local time, 12 or 24 hours, as set in the game's clock window; its tooltip shows both the realm and the local time) followed by the day and date that time belongs to (the realm's date while the clock shows realm time; "21:04  Tue 13 Oct", or "9:04 PM  Tue 13 Oct" on a 12-hour clock) with a calendar button at its right end that opens the calendar like `/calendar` (in a fight chat says the calendar waits instead; the game's own calendar button stays put away). While calendar invites wait for your answer the button carries their count in its corner and its tooltip says how many; where the realm turns the calendar off there is no button. While you level, a fifth row adds your XP per hour this session beside the time to your next level at that pace, and a sixth your gold per hour (red while you're spending); they wait a minute for the numbers to settle, carry on through a `/reload` (a new login starts them again), and the XP ones hide at the level cap and while XP gain is locked (the gold per hour row then moves up under the time, as a row with nothing to show is closed up). Beside the gold rate it can show what your auction sales came to this session, "AH +12g 5s" (from the sales ledger, the sales the auction house told you of rather than old ones first read from the mail; it carries on through a `/reload` and starts again with the rates; off until you turn it on). On Retail, the gold row can show the Great Vault beside your gold, "Vault 3/9": the slots unlocked this week of all the rows the vault shows (raids, dungeons and world; rated PvP where the vault has no world row), or "Vault reward waiting" in the accent while last week's reward waits to be picked (off until you turn it on, on the Game windows page; it updates as the vault does, and stays hidden where the vault has no slots or the game can't say). Its tooltip lists each row's unlocked slots and the progress to the next ("Raids 2/3 unlocked, next 4/6"), and a click opens the vault (not in a fight). A seventh row can show your DPS in the last fight, from the game's damage meter (off until you turn it on; it updates when a fight ends, and stays hidden where the game keeps it secret), and, in a group, your place in that fight's DPS ("#2 of 5", the place the game's own meter shows; also off until you turn it on). Each number explains itself on hover: durability lists each worn item under 100 %, most worn first; the free count each bag's slots used (a soul bag or quiver named as such); gold what you earned and spent since you logged in; fps and latency your home and world latency and Vanguard's memory (not read in a fight); the per-hour rates and time to level how long the session has run, the XP gained and the gold made in it; your DPS and place the last fight's length (in a fight, that it updates when the fight ends). Out of a fight a click on durability opens the character window and a click on the free count or gold opens your bags (in a fight nothing opens and chat says to try after it; each tooltip ends with what its click does), and a right-click on a per-hour rate or the time to level starts the session's rates counting again from now (the gold tooltip's earned and spent still count from the login). Each part can be turned off at once except the clock: its switch (**Clock (reload)**) applies at the next reload, because the game's own minimap clock and calendar button are put away (or left) only at login, and a Situations override can't record it; one the game can't answer stays hidden. The strip is always shown unless you pick another visibility for it (On mouseover, In combat, Hidden or the action bars' row of conditions); so are the XP and reputation bars, which share one setting. At a larger Text size the strip grows wider and its rows taller in step, so the longest texts (a 12-hour time with the date, "100% durability" beside the free count) still fit side by side; smaller text keeps the strip at its usual size. The strip also widens when a row's blocks need more room than its width (a longer translation, a big gold count). The quest tracker sits just under the strip with room for all seven rows, and moves down with the taller rows at a larger Text size (after a fight, if you change it in one; a spot you picked with `/vg unlock` stays).

## Broker strip

Other addons' data feeds (LibDataBroker data sources, the kind info-bar addons show: gold trackers, durability, guild counts and so on) in a thin strip along the top edge of the screen, left of center, movable with `/vg unlock`. Pick the feeds under Map → Data from other addons (up to four, in the order picked); each shows its icon (with the crop and tint the feed gives) and text in a slot as wide as they need (48 to 200 wide; a slot grows while you play but never shrinks, so a ticking figure doesn't make the strip jump), its own tooltip on hover (below the strip in the top half of the screen, above it in the bottom half), and takes your clicks. The strip and its icons grow with Text size, and every slot is measured again when it changes (so a slot widened at a larger size narrows again). Launchers stay in the minimap drawer. Vanguard doesn't bundle LibDataBroker: the addons that share feeds load it, so with none of them the strip stays hidden.

The **Coordinates under the minimap** switch (`/vg` → Map) applies at once: the band under the map, its text and the ticker that reads your position go and come back with no reload. The minimap's shape, its corner icons and the info strip's clock still take a reload (the shape builds the disc, ring and rim at login; the corner icons and the clock put away the game's own buttons once, and there is no way to give them back mid-session).

## Minimap drawer

Other addons' round minimap buttons are gathered into a drawer beside the map; the small "..." button on the map's left edge opens and closes it (it only shows once there's a button to gather; its tooltip counts them, "Addon buttons (5)", and Escape closes the drawer too). The buttons sit in their addons' name order, each in a 31 unit cell (the size those addons build them at, so the round ring keeps its place on the icon), and the drawer opens beside the map on the side with room: left of it, or right of it when the map sits at the screen's left edge, and never off the screen. A button an addon makes later, when you turn it on in that addon's settings or it loads on demand, joins the drawer at once (after the fight, in one); a protected button is left on the map, and a button its addon has hidden takes no place in the drawer and is not counted. On WoW Forever the game's event link button (it shows only while the game has one) sits small on the map's top-left corner under the dungeon difficulty, not under the faded backdrop where it would take clicks unseen. It needs Vanguard's Minimap module: with that off (or turned off for another minimap addon), the buttons stay on the game's map, and its row under Modules says so. Other addons that place buttons and pins around the map ask Vanguard for the map's shape: square, or round when it is built round; with the module off, round (the game's map), or what another minimap addon loaded before Vanguard says.

## Waypoints

`/way 45.2 61.8` puts a waypoint on the map you're on and the game's own navigation arrow points to it; `/way Elwynn Forest 42 65 Inn` names a zone (any case, or a unique start of its name) and a title, `/way #1429 42 65` a map by its id, and `/way clear` removes it. Commas work too (`45.2, 61.8`, or `45,61`); a comma between two digits is a decimal comma as long as two coordinates are still to read (`45,2 61,8` is 45.2 and 61.8). `/way` is Vanguard's only while no other addon (a waypoint addon, say) has it; `/vg way` always works. While the world map is open, a line along its bottom shows your coordinates and the cursor's. With OpenMap (an optional companion addon that draws one seamless map) loaded, the line shows only yours, since OpenMap's map isn't the zone the cursor would be read against, and the Map page says OpenMap is drawing the map. The Map page's OpenMap card credits its author, jiforever (jigrobbulus on CurseForge), whether or not it is installed. The floating pin and distance the game draws over the world for a waypoint or a supertracked quest take Vanguard's pin in your accent and the theme font (a party member's portrait and the corpse's tombstone stay the game's); the pin's place, distance and fading stay the game's own. All three are on by default, under Map. Ctrl-click on the world map puts a waypoint down (the game's own); the game leaves its arrow off for it, so Vanguard turns the arrow on (switch under Map → Waypoints, on by default). The last eight waypoints are kept, each with the title `/way` gave it (`Inn · Elwynn Forest 42.0, 65.0`; a later Ctrl-click on the same spot keeps the title): the Map page lists them as "Go to ..." buttons, `/way recent` lists them and `/way recent <n>` goes back to one. Sharing a waypoint and clearing it on arrival are the game's own (the Waypoints card says to Shift-click the pin on the map to share it). A Clear waypoint button sits on the world map while there is a waypoint, and on the Map page.

Data bars, Broker strip and HUD text switch off and on at once under `/vg` → Modules, with no reload: the game's own status bars come back, the broker strip goes, and the HUD fonts return to the game's; only the damage-number font waits for a reload. The Experience bar and Reputation bar switches apply at once too.
