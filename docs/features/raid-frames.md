# Raid frames and click casting

What Vanguard does here, in full. This page is part of the behaviour spec; the [README](../../README.md) has the overview and [docs/README.md](../README.md) lists every page.

## Raid frames

Raid frames (built for healers):

- Laid out in zones that never overlap: name at the top, status (missing health, Dead, Ghost, Offline) under it, the missing-buff mark in the top-right corner, debuffs bottom left, your HoTs bottom right, the power bar along the bottom, and the ready check, a summon or the resurrection big in the center (one at a time, in that order). Default tile 100 x 48, drawn smaller in bigger raids. The raid frames' handle (`/vg unlock`) has Scale and Opacity: the tiles are drawn at that Scale times the group's compact size and at that Opacity (a Scale above 100% grows the grid for up to 25 players; a bigger raid keeps its compact size so it stays clear of the main tanks and raid tools, and a smaller Scale still shrinks it); the Healing layout's 115% is that Scale. The missing-health text grows and shrinks with the tile but never drops under the theme's smallest text size on screen (10 px at 100% text size); on a grid drawn smaller (more than 10 players) it moves to the right end of the name row to keep that size, on one line about 32 units wide (a narrower tile gives it at most 40% of its width) that ends in "..." if the word is longer ("Dead" and "-760" fit; "-1440" and "Offline" are cut on the smallest tiles), which leaves the name seven or eight letters on a 25 or 40 player tile, so it never wraps into the icon strip.
- Readable at a glance: class colors softened into the panel color by default (a near-white class such as Priest is dimmed to a light gray, so the light name on it keeps 3:1 contrast), with a 1-pixel line in the full class color along each tile's top so close colors (shaman and mage blues, rogue and paladin yellows) still tell apart (or dark bars with class-colored names, or bright class colors), outlined names that end in "..." when too long, and a dark bar on the dead and offline. In instanced combat, where the game keeps health secret from addons, the bars still fill and the missing-health text stays: the game works out the number ("-1234", blank at full health) and Vanguard only shows it, the same look as out of combat.
- Auras: the first debuff you can dispel, then up to two others (boss debuffs included); your HoTs, lit while they're in their refresh window; no timer numbers on these small icons. A debuff you can dispel washes the tile in its type's color from the bottom and colors its edge. Every debuff icon carries its type's letter in its corner (M, C, D or P) with a small mark beside it (diamond, circle, square or triangle), so the type reads without its color. Up to three small indicator icons run down the tile's right edge under the missing-buff mark (on short tiles they continue in a column to the left); **Right edge, top**, **Right edge, second** and **Right edge, third** pick what each shows (None, External defensive, Big defensive, Important buff, Your buffs, Crowd control, Dispellable debuff), after a reload.
- Healing: click casting and hover keybindings (a hover key can carry Shift, Ctrl or Alt: while the pointer is over a frame, Shift-Q casts on that player), incoming heals and shields, aggro strip, missing-buff marker (hidden while the game hides auras, so it never cries wolf), an accent edge on your target, a faint wash under the mouse, and out of combat the player's tooltip while you point at a tile (raid tiles, and the main tank, party pet and party target tiles; a pull hides it, and in a fight none shows).
- Status: range fade, raid target icons (10 wide, centered on the tile's top edge like the leader crown, so they stay off the name; the same switch shows them on the unit frames), ready check (a check, cross or three dots in the theme colors), resurrection marker, the game's summon icon while someone summons a player (pending, accepted or declined; it shows instead of the resurrection marker, under the same switch, **Mark players being resurrected or summoned**), and a gold crown for the leader and a small diamond for assistants. While the ready check, resurrection or summon mark shows in the middle, the missing-health text steps aside.
- Roles: a small shield for tanks and a cross for healers before the name; damage dealers get no mark. The raid's main tank gets the shield in your accent color, and the main assist in gray.
- Groups as columns (the default) or rows: columns put each group's five players in a column, with the groups side by side; rows put each group's five in one row, with the groups stacked upward, so a big healing raid is wide and short. Pick it with **Groups as** on the Raid frames page in `/vg`; a change takes a reload. Whatever you pick, a raid of more than 10 stacks as rows unless you turn off **Stack big raids at the left**.
- Preview without a group: **Preview with sample players** at the bottom of the Raid frames page (or `/vg preview party`, `10`, `25`, `40`, `off`) fills the tiles with made-up players (classes, roles, missing health, one dead and one offline) as a party of five or a raid of 10, 25 or 40, where your raid frames sit, drawn smaller for the bigger raids as the real grid is. A new width, height, style, sorting or Groups as shows in the preview at once, while the real frames still take a reload. The preview is never saved: it ends when you turn it off, join a group, enter combat or reload, and it won't start in a group or a fight. The party preview shows the raid tiles, as your party sees them with **Use them for your party too** on (the default); the separate party frames used with that switch off aren't previewed. The real frames are left alone throughout.

## Click casting

Click casting (`/vg` → Click casting), set up without typing a spell:

- **Starter layouts** (WoW Forever): the **Starter layout...** dropdown at the top of the page offers every class "Damage or tank", and a Priest, Druid, Paladin or Shaman also "Healer: click to select" and "Healer: click to heal". Each layout lists its spells by id, best first, and binds the name your client gives each, so it works in every client language. Picking one binds, on each click, the best spell your character knows that an earlier click didn't take (a level 1 priest gets Lesser Heal on Middle, a level 60 one Flash Heal; Heal goes on Middle until Flash Heal is learned rather than on Shift + Left too), and leaves a click empty while it knows none of its spells. Every layout puts Focus on Alt + Middle click and Assist on Ctrl + Shift + Middle click.
  - **Healer: click to select**: Left click is Target and Right click the unit menu (the two clicks the game's own click profile covers by default, which the client needs to run a Target or Unit menu click at all) and the main heal is on Middle click, a third heal on Shift + Middle where the class has one (Prayer of Healing, Holy Shock, Riptide), the dispels on Ctrl, and on Alt a shield, blessing or Swiftmend and the resurrection (Revive for a druid, Ancestral Spirit for a shaman). Emergency spells (Lay on Hands, Blessing of Protection, Rebirth, Nature's Swiftness) sit two modifiers away, on Ctrl + Shift + Left and Right.
  - **Healer: click to heal**: the main heal on Left click, the Shift + Left heal on Right click and the Shift + Right one on Middle, with Target on Shift + Left click and the unit menu on Shift + Right click. The game runs those two only on clicks its own Click Casting window binds, so while it doesn't, picking it shows a guide over the layout row instead of applying it: what is still to bind there, in the game's words ("Still to bind in the game's window: Target on Shift + Left click.", and Open menu on Shift + Right click), checked again each time the page opens (where Vanguard can't read what the game binds to such a click, the line says so and asks you to check it there), and **Open the game's window**, **Put heals on Left and Right** (which applies it whatever the game's window binds; the clicks still to bind do nothing until they are, and the guide says so) and **Cancel**. Vanguard counts a click as bound only when it can read the game's binding for it and it is that interaction. The guide belongs to the talent group's list it was picked on. A client without the game's click bindings applies it at once.
  - **Damage or tank**: Target on Left and the menu on Right, with a class's helpful clicks: Power Word: Shield and Renew on Shift for a priest, Cleanse on Ctrl + Left and the emergency row for a paladin, Rebirth on the emergency row for a druid, Lesser Healing Wave on Shift + Left for a shaman, the buffs for a mage (Arcane Intellect, Remove Lesser Curse) and a warlock (Unending Breath), and Target, the menu, Focus and Assist only for a warrior, rogue or hunter.
  - The other two layouts keep Target on plain Left and the menu on plain Right, so they show the same guide when the game's Click Casting window doesn't bind those (say, after you moved them to Shift for Click to heal), with **Put the layout on** in place of Put heals.
  - A layout replaces every mouse binding of the list the page shows (your hover keys stay); when those aren't the ones a layout made (your own, or a layout's you changed since; an offer you took on a list still as the layout made it counts as the layout's), the page asks first, when it is about to apply ("Replace your own click-casting mouse bindings with this starter layout? Undo puts them back."). It keeps the list from before the first layout: **Undo** (on the row "These bindings came from the starter layout ..." over the layout row) asks first, then puts that list back, hover keys included, and changes made since are lost; trying the other layouts in between doesn't lose it. In a fight the list changes at once and the frames take it when the fight ends. Clear all, Import and Copy to (on the list copied onto) make a list that is no longer a layout's, so its Undo and offers go.
  - **Better spells**: when you learn a spell a layout's click would take over what it has (a better heal, or one for a click the layout had to leave empty), chat says so once (an offer that only appears because you used another, such as Heal freed for Shift + Left by Flash Heal on Middle, shows on the page) ("Click casting: you can put Flash Heal on Middle click now. /vg > Click casting offers it.") and the page shows a row for it ("Middle click could cast Flash Heal instead of Heal.") with **Use Flash Heal** and **Keep as it is**. Nothing changes by itself. A click you bound to something the layout doesn't list, or emptied yourself, is yours and gets no offer, a better spell you haven't learned yet is never offered down to a worse one, and Keep as it is keeps that spell from being offered on that click again. No offers come while the game's click bindings are in use or another raid frame or click-casting addon is loaded.
  - When another addon draws the raid frames or does click casting (VuhDo, Grid2, Cell, EllesmereUI's raid frames or Clique, from Coexist's list), the page says "You use VuhDo for raid frames. Vanguard's click casting works only on its own frames, so no starter layout is offered." and the dropdown isn't shown, whatever you answered on the Modules page's Other addons card.
  - On other clients the page keeps one button with your class's healing layout (Priest, Druid, Paladin, Shaman; Mage and Warlock get their buffs), "Click to select" in the same way, laid over the bindings you have (the keys it names are replaced, the others kept), with no Undo and no offers, whatever other addons are loaded.
  - `/vg probe` lists the name your client gives each spell id the layouts use ("Click-casting starter layout spells"), so one run shows any id the client doesn't know.
- **The menu-click notice**: a saved list that still has the older shape (a spell or macro on plain Right click and the Unit menu on Middle) is not rewritten. Where the game's click profile doesn't cover Middle click, the Unit menu there opens nothing, so the Click casting page shows a notice above the layout row (the starter layout control, Clear all, More modifiers): "Middle click can't open the unit menu on your raid frames...", with **Swap them** and **Dismiss**. Swap them puts the menu on Right click and what was on Right click on Middle, and changes nothing else; chat says which lists it changed (or that it changed none, when the notice had gone out of date), and in a fight the change is kept and applies when the fight ends. Dismiss keeps the notice away for that character, every talent group included. The notice shows only where the game's click bindings exist, fail Middle because they don't cover it, and would let both Right (the menu) and Middle (the spell) run after the swap (a game Spell on Middle, or the game's own Spell on Right, keeps it away); never while the game's bindings are in use; and it goes as soon as the shape does. It is checked again whenever the window opens, so it comes back or goes if the stamp or the game's bindings changed meanwhile. Swap them fixes every talent group whose list has the shape (on WoW Forever both groups start from the same old list), and the notice says when it will fix the other group too. A healer whose list has the shape is also told once in chat at login, since the page opens on the tab it showed last and the notice sits on the first one.
- **When the game's own click bindings get in the way**: where the game has click bindings of its own, it runs a Target or Unit menu binding only on a click those cover (plain Left and Right unless you change them in its Click Casting window) and never from a hover key. Such a binding is kept, but its grid cell is drawn in the warning colour (hovering it says why), its hover-key row says "does nothing: the game won't run it from a key", and `/vg binds` adds "(does nothing: ...)" with the reason. Whether the game covers a click is judged by its exact modifiers (a Target on Ctrl + Left does not cover Shift + Left). Vanguard reads the game's profile with `GetProfileInfo` and decodes each modifier mask with `GetStringFromModifiers`. Two more reasons are given for a mouse binding. If the game's profile has a Spell, Macro or Pet action on the same click (the exact same button and modifiers), the game runs its own binding first and never reads Vanguard's: the tooltip says "Does nothing: the game's own Click Casting window binds a spell or macro to this click, and that runs instead." If the game moved Target or Open menu to another click, the client runs the binding of the click that interaction stands for (Left for Target, Right for the menu), so every Vanguard binding on the moved click says "the game's own Click Casting window puts Target on this click, so it runs your Left click binding instead" (or the unit menu and Right click). A game binding whose modifier text Vanguard can't read, or whose interaction it doesn't know, gives no warning, since it only warns when it is sure; a client without the game's click bindings never warns.
- **Clear all** (beside the starter layout control) asks "Clear every click-casting binding for this spec?" first and empties that list only on Clear. On WoW Forever it names the talent group instead ("...for Primary talents?") and empties that group's list only.
- **The grid**: mouse buttons down the side, Plain / Shift / Ctrl / Alt across the top (More modifiers adds the combinations, and they show by themselves while a binding uses one, as every starter layout's Assist does; Fewer modifiers hides them again); each cell shows the bound spell's icon. A column's name that runs longer than its column wraps onto a second line. Click a cell to pick from your own spellbook, right-click to clear it, or drop a spell from the spellbook or a bar onto it (a lower rank you drop binds that rank, the top rank binds by name).
- **The picker** lists what you can cast on friends in groups (Heals, Protect & cleanse, Buffs, Other; your class's spells are sorted by id, so the groups hold in every client language), with a search box and the spell's tooltip on hover; a fight closes it, and one opened during a fight is a mouse-only popup, so it never holds your keys in combat, and it reads keys again when the fight ends if it is still open. A spell with ranks binds as its highest rank unless you open its ranks and pick one (for downranking); then the cell says "Flash Heal (Rank 3)". Under the spells: Target, Unit menu, Focus, Assist, a macro, or a typed spell name.
- **Hover keys**: Add key (at the top of its card, with a hint beside it until you have one), press the key (Shift + Q, the wheel...), pick the spell (not in a fight: Add key does nothing until the fight ends, and a fight starting while it waits for your key puts it back, so no key of the fight is swallowed); the row says when the key is also an action-bar binding ("also Action bar 1 slot 2"), which it keeps doing off the frames. Under the list, **See all your keys** opens the Action bars page on its Your keys tab, for what every key does now.
- **Try it**: hover the sample tile and press or click; it says what would happen ("Shift + Right click -> Renew on you") without casting. While the pointer is on the tile it keeps the keys it tests from the game, so a key you try casts and moves nothing; Escape and the Shift, Ctrl and Alt keys still reach the game, and the keys are the game's again as soon as the pointer leaves. In a fight the tile does not read keys at all (it still reads clicks and the wheel), so movement and your keybinds keep working with the pointer on it, and it reads keys again when the fight ends if the pointer is still on it.
- **Share**: Export and Import act on the list you are on (this talent group's, or this spec's): switch to the group first to import a string made on another one. Import checks the whole string first and only then replaces your bindings, so a string that is empty, holds nothing usable, has more than 200 bindings or a text over 255 characters is refused with the reason and your bindings stay as they were. Where the client has specs, **Copy to...** copies the list to another one (on WoW Forever, **Copy to Secondary talents** or **Copy to Primary talents**). If that one already has bindings it asks "Replace the bindings of Secondary talents with these?" first, and a list is never copied onto itself. The page is refreshed after a copy. Bindings are per character and spec, and on WoW Forever, where dual spec is the talent group, per talent group: Primary and Secondary talents each keep their own list, and the page's line over the grid names the one you are on. The group the client last named is remembered, so a client that doesn't name it yet at login applies and edits that group's list, never the one that predates the groups; it is checked again at each loading screen and on a talent group or specialization change. The first time a character logs in with talent groups, the one list it had before is copied to each group that has none, once (nothing is marked done while there is no old list and no group in use). That old list stays where it was, so an older Vanguard still finds it, but edits made in an older version after a rollback are not carried forward. Changes apply at once, or when the fight ends if you're in one. A spell bound by name whose saved name this client doesn't know (you switched the client language) is cast under the client's name for its spell id; the saved name is left alone, so switching back keeps working.
- **The game's click bindings** (where the client has its own click casting): a switch at the bottom of the page, off by default, uses the game's instead. Vanguard's bindings and hover keys then rest (kept for when you switch back, and `/vg binds` says so), and the bindings you set in the game's Click Casting window (the page's button opens it, out of combat; in a fight chat says the window waits) work on the raid and unit frames. The switch applies out of combat; turned on in a fight, it waits for the fight to end.

## Raid tools

For the group's leader and assistants, a small bar at the top left with a ready check, a 10-second pull timer (right-click cancels it), a role check and Raid (turns your party into a raid), the eight raid target icons (click to mark your target; the last button clears it) and the eight world markers (click to place or remove one; right-click clears it; the last button clears them all). Ready and Pull are secure buttons that run the game's own `/readycheck` and `/countdown` commands, since the client may reserve those calls for secure code; the raid target icons are the game's own secure raid target buttons for the same reason (clicking a set icon again keeps it). The text buttons showing share one width, wide enough for the longest of their labels, and the bar widens with them; Raid is hidden in a raid, so its label asks for no room there and the bar stays as wide as its icon rows. It shows and hides out of combat.
