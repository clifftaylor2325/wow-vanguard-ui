# Raid frames and click casting

What Vanguard does here, in full. This page is part of the behaviour spec; the [README](../../README.md) has the overview and [docs/README.md](../README.md) lists every page.

## Raid frames

Raid frames (built for healers):

- Laid out in zones that never overlap: name at the top, status (missing health, Dead, Ghost, Offline) under it, the missing-buff mark in the top-right corner, debuffs bottom left, your HoTs bottom right, the power bar along the bottom, and the ready check and resurrection big in the center. Default tile 100 x 48, drawn smaller in bigger raids. The missing-health text grows and shrinks with the tile but never drops under the theme's smallest text size on screen (10 px at 100% text size); on a grid drawn smaller (more than 10 players) it moves to the right end of the name row to keep that size.
- Readable at a glance: class colors softened into the panel color by default (a near-white class such as Priest is dimmed to a light gray, so the light name on it keeps 3:1 contrast), with a 1-pixel line in the full class color along each tile's top so close colors (shaman and mage blues, rogue and paladin yellows) still tell apart (or dark bars with class-colored names, or bright class colors), outlined names that end in "..." when too long, and a dark bar on the dead and offline. In instanced combat, where the game keeps health secret from addons, the bars still fill and the missing-health text hides.
- Auras: the first debuff you can dispel, then up to two others (boss debuffs included); your HoTs, lit while they're in their refresh window; no timer numbers on these small icons. A debuff you can dispel washes the tile in its type's color from the bottom and colors its edge. Every debuff icon carries its type's letter in its corner (M, C, D or P) with a small mark beside it (diamond, circle, square or triangle), so the type reads without its color.
- Healing: click casting and hover keybindings (a hover key can carry Shift, Ctrl or Alt: while the pointer is over a frame, Shift-Q casts on that player), incoming heals and shields, aggro strip, missing-buff marker (hidden while the game hides auras, so it never cries wolf), an accent edge on your target, a faint wash under the mouse.
- Status: range fade, raid target icons, ready check (a check, cross or three dots in the theme colors), resurrection marker, and a gold crown for the leader and a small diamond for assistants. While the ready check or resurrection mark shows in the middle, the missing-health text steps aside.
- Roles: a small shield for tanks and a cross for healers before the name; damage dealers get no mark. The raid's main tank gets the shield in your accent color, and the main assist in gray.
- Groups as columns (the default) or rows: columns put each group's five players in a column, with the groups side by side; rows put each group's five in one row, with the groups stacked upward, so a big healing raid is wide and short. Pick it with **Groups as** on the Raid frames page in `/vg`; a change takes a reload. Whatever you pick, a raid of more than 10 stacks as rows unless you turn off **Stack big raids at the left**.
- Preview without a group: **Preview with sample players** at the bottom of the Raid frames page (or `/vg preview party`, `10`, `25`, `40`, `off`) fills the tiles with made-up players (classes, roles, missing health, one dead and one offline) as a party of five or a raid of 10, 25 or 40, where your raid frames sit, drawn smaller for the bigger raids as the real grid is. A new width, height, style, sorting or Groups as shows in the preview at once, while the real frames still take a reload. The preview is never saved: it ends when you turn it off, join a group, enter combat or reload, and it won't start in a group or a fight. The party preview shows the raid tiles, as your party sees them with **Use them for your party too** on (the default); the separate party frames used with that switch off aren't previewed. The real frames are left alone throughout.

## Click casting

Click casting (`/vg` → Click casting), set up without typing a spell:

- **Starter layout**: one button fills the grid with your class's healing layout (Priest, Druid, Paladin, Shaman; Mage and Warlock get their buffs). Spells bind by name, so one you haven't learned yet starts working the day you learn it. Left click stays Target and middle click the unit menu. Where the game has click bindings of its own, it runs a Target or Unit menu binding only on a click those cover (plain Left and Right unless you change them in its Click Casting window) and never from a hover key; such a binding is kept, but its grid cell is drawn in the warning colour (hovering it says why), its hover-key row says "does nothing: the game won't run it from a key", and `/vg binds` adds "(does nothing: ...)" with the reason.
- **The grid**: mouse buttons down the side, Plain / Shift / Ctrl / Alt across the top (More modifiers adds the combinations); each cell shows the bound spell's icon. Click a cell to pick from your own spellbook, right-click to clear it, or drop a spell from the spellbook or a bar onto it.
- **The picker** lists what you can cast on friends in groups (Heals, Protect & cleanse, Buffs, Other), with a search box and the spell's tooltip on hover; a fight closes it, so it never holds your keys in combat. A spell with ranks binds as its highest rank unless you open its ranks and pick one (for downranking); then the cell says "Flash Heal (Rank 3)". Under the spells: Target, Unit menu, Focus, Assist, a macro, or a typed spell name.
- **Hover keys**: Add key (at the top of its card, with a hint beside it until you have one), press the key (Shift + Q, the wheel...), pick the spell; the row says when the key is also an action-bar binding ("also Action bar 1 slot 2"), which it keeps doing off the frames.
- **Try it**: hover the sample tile and press or click; it says what would happen ("Shift + Right click -> Renew on you") without casting. While the pointer is on the tile it keeps the keys it tests from the game, so a key you try casts and moves nothing; Escape and the Shift, Ctrl and Alt keys still reach the game, and the keys are the game's again as soon as the pointer leaves.
- **Share**: Export and Import the bindings as a string (Import checks the whole string first and only then replaces your bindings, so a string that is empty, holds nothing usable, has more than 200 bindings or a text over 255 characters is refused with the reason and your bindings stay as they were), and copy them to another spec where the client has specs. Bindings are per character and spec; changes apply at once, or when the fight ends if you're in one.
- **The game's click bindings** (where the client has its own click casting): a switch at the bottom of the page, off by default, uses the game's instead. Vanguard's bindings and hover keys then rest (kept for when you switch back, and `/vg binds` says so), and the bindings you set in the game's Click Casting window (the page's button opens it, out of combat; in a fight chat says the window waits) work on the raid and unit frames. The switch applies out of combat; turned on in a fight, it waits for the fight to end.

## Raid tools

For the group's leader and assistants, a small bar at the top left with a ready check, a 10-second pull timer (right-click cancels it), a role check and Raid (turns your party into a raid), the eight raid target icons (click to mark your target; the last button clears it) and the eight world markers (click to place or remove one; right-click clears it; the last button clears them all). It shows and hides out of combat.
