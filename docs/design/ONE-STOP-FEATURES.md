# Vanguard Can Win Forever's Non-Combat Game

Written 2026-10-10 against commit `b032e6f` (v0.5.0), from public web sources listed at the end. Nothing here has been verified in a Forever client. The feature numbers (1 to 17) are the ones the GitHub issues use.

Vanguard should define "one-stop shop" as the complete **non-combat Forever journey** (levelling, professions, economy, group-forming, raid prep) in one coherent UI that skins Blizzard's combat tools and coexists with the best standalone addons. It should not try to replace Details, DBM or WeakAuras. Forever shares Mainline's UI architecture and the Midnight addon restrictions, so features that compute on live combat data are display-only at best ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/addons-in-wow-forever-blizzard-devs-just-addressed-the-big-question/)). EllesmereUI v9.4 already ships a broad Forever suite, including a threat meter and flight timer ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/wow-forever-players-can-now-build-their-perfect-ui-with-ellesmereui/)). Vanguard's lane is where EllesmereUI is thin and players are underserved: auction and economy tooling, vendor and cross-character memory, quest and levelling aids, and group-forming in a game with no Dungeon Finder. The five do-first items are a **settings-loss guard**, a **ruleset-safe data-key audit**, **client-data quest guidance with a "new Forever quest" mark**, a **coexistence manager** and a **chat-based LFG board**. Evidence is thin. Reddit, YouTube and Wowhead were unreachable, so demand is inferred from what authors build, download counts and issue titles, not from player statements. Nothing here has been verified in a Forever client.

## Why breadth outside combat beats parity inside it

Three facts set the strategy. First, the restrictions are fixed: Blizzard says Forever uses the Midnight rules and does not want addons to have "too much power and control" ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/addons-in-wow-forever-blizzard-devs-just-addressed-the-big-question/)). Tainted code can store and format secret values but not compare or compute on them ([Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Secret_Values)). Second, EllesmereUI is the only suite with confirmed, detailed Forever support, at v9.4 on Oct 7 ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/ellesmereui)). Third, no suite bundles the Forever-native reference tools (dungeon journal, talent planner, levelling, quest data). Icy Veins still recommends eight separate specialist addons beside EllesmereUI ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/set-up-your-wow-forever-ui-before-launch-with-these-9-addons/)); that this leaves a gap is my inference from feature lists, not a measured finding. Guides tell players to "pick one main UI suite" and one bag addon ([Overgear](https://overgear.com/guides/wow-forever/best-addons-for-every-activity/)), so a suite must also behave well next to what players already run.

The Forever-only addons with the biggest numbers show where players pull: ClassicUI Forever 87.5K ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/classicui-forever)), Forever Guide 48.6K ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/forever-guide)), Forever Quest Tint 32.7K ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/forever-quest-tint)), Forever Quest Pins 28.6K ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/forever-quest-pins)) and What's Training 26.3K ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/whats-training-forever)). All are lifetime totals from roughly four weeks of beta. ClassicUI's listing also covers Retail, so part of its total may not be Forever. Economy addons by contrast sit in the tens to low thousands, which suggests an unmet but unproven need. The ranking below weighs five factors: number of independent sources, Vanguard's gap, effort, restriction risk and data risk. It is judgement, not measurement.

One lane needs verification before it can be marketed. Cell has no recent file and VuhDo lists no Forever file ([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/cell), [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/vuhdo)). Clique and Grid2 were reported broken on build 69913, by a weak source ([wowforeverguides](https://wowforeverguides.com/addons)). If Vanguard's raid frames and click casting work on a real client, they are a moat for healers. That is conditional on the secure-snippet bug noted below.

## Ranked features: five to build first, then conditional work

Effort: S under a week, M one to three weeks, L longer (solo developer, my estimate). "Check" means in-game verification is required. Evidence labels: Warcraft Wiki API facts are confirmed for retail 12.x and merely listed for Forever 1.60.1; "reported" means one tester or guide; all demand readings are inferred from supply, since no player statements were reachable. Earlier EllesmereUI lessons (relationship movers, hardened profile strings, harness secret modes, first-run window) still stand and are not repeated.

## What Blizzard and EllesmereUI already cover

Do not propose these. Blizzard's built-ins: Edit Mode, click casting, a Personal Resource Display ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/world-of-warcraft-forever-ui-addons-setup-guide)), combined bags ([Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/guides/ui-settings-guide)), a damage meter and Cooldown Manager, which Blizzard says are coming to Forever, with the manager off by default and still adding classes (reported by [a guide](https://wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-built-in-ui-setup/)). EllesmereUI's Forever additions: threat meter, flight timer, heal and spell-cost prediction, faction indicators, level-difficulty colours, dispel-type borders, character-sheet stats and weapon DPS, pet power bar and pet happiness, last-name display ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/wow-forever-players-can-now-build-their-perfect-ui-with-ellesmereui/)). Later releases add a five-second-rule spark, a loot feed and auto-uprank (from the earlier EllesmereUI research in `docs/design/ELLESMERE-LESSONS.md`, which read its release notes). Vanguard already has its own pet happiness, five-second spark, threat %, flight timer, swing timer, XP/hour, talent planner, auction suite and quest UI, so none of those belong on the list.

Specialists that players already run: Questie and TomTom (quest pins, waypoints), Leatrix Plus and Maps, RestedXP (routes, with Forever routes for levels 35 to 60 only after launch, per [Kami Labs](https://kami-labs.fr/en/wow-forever/restedxp-wow-forever-guide-leveling/)), Baganator, Auctionator, Platynator and Plater, Details, DBM and BigWigs, all with Forever builds on CurseForge. Vanguard should interoperate with these, as its Auctionator-compatible API and list import already do, not out-feature them. Item 3 uses only client data and leaves Questie's pins and tracker alone when Questie is loaded, as Vanguard's tracker already does.

## Blocked or risky features and the clean alternative

| Feature | Why it is blocked or risky | Clean alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Combat-log parsing (meters, GTFO-style alerts) | Registering combat-log events errors since 12.0.0 ([Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_12.0.0/API_changes)); `CombatLogGetCurrentEventInfo` reportedly absent ([kit](https://github.com/Thunderz96/forever-addon-kit)). | Skin and read the game's damage meter; recap out of combat (item 12). |
| WeakAuras-style triggers, rotation helpers | Port stalled or absent ([ExitLag](https://www.exitlag.com/blog/wow-forever-addons/)); the Forever fork reportedly never fires combat triggers (weak source, [wowforeverguides](https://wowforeverguides.com/addons)). | Display-only trackers that pass duration objects to Blizzard widgets; out-of-combat checks; reskin the game's manager. |
| Threat | Sources conflict (see below). | Own-target % today; group view only after the test. |
| Automatic AH posting, buying, cancelling | Blizzard's limits: competitors prepare each action for one click ([Hammerprice](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/hammerprice)). | Keep Vanguard's rule: it never posts, buys or cancels. |
| Questie, QuestieDB or cmangos data | GPLv3; cmangos notes extra copyrighted material. Forever Guide pairs "All Rights Reserved" with QuestieDB credits, a pattern to avoid (this is an observation, not legal advice). | Client APIs, trainer/vendor-style memory, your own extracts with credits, opt-in observation, original content. |
| Live interrupt tracker, instance assignment sync | Interrupt restriction predicted but unconfirmed ([Kami Labs](https://kami-labs.fr/en/wow-classic/wow-forever-addons-restreints-comme-sur-midnight/)); addon messages restricted in instances. | Personal "your kick is ready" icon; meter Interrupts category after the fact. |

Also defer: Hardcore tooling (Blizzard says winter, not launch, per [PC Games Hardware](https://www.pcgameshardware.de/WoW-Forever-Spiel-78891/News/Forever-mit-Hardcore-Modus-1553823/)), accessibility modes (no evidence either way), and a load-on-demand module split (EllesmereUI ships standalone modules, but nothing shows Vanguard's users want it; modules already toggle). Stay free: Blizzard's addon development policy (undated on the page read) requires addons to be free and bars in-game ads ([Blizzard forum](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/ui-add-on-development-policy/24534)).

## Open questions and the in-game test that resolves each

| Question | Conflict in the research | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Are SavedVariables fixed? | Data Protect's author says 1.60.1 loads settings again; Details reports a vanished window after the "October update" (cause unknown, [GitHub](https://api.github.com/search/issues?q=repo:Tercioo/Details-Damage-Meter+forever&per_page=30)); Blizzard has not replied. | Change a Vanguard setting, exit the game (not `/reload`), relog; repeat for per-character data, and again on the launch build. |
| Does Forever's group finder list premade groups? | Tavern: the LFG window only organizes; TimeSaver: you post and browse listings by role; EllesmereUI dropped retail features because the finder "has no premade group list". | Post a listing on one character and search from another; dump `C_LFGList` activities and results. Decides whether item 5 is chat-only or a hybrid. |
| Can threat work in combat? | The kit and one guide say threat is hidden; ForeverMeter ships threat from `UnitDetailedThreatSituation`; the wiki tags that call secret-when-restricted and untainted-allowed, with no combat rule stated ([Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_UnitDetailedThreatSituation)). | In open world, a dungeon and a boss pull, print `C_Secrets.ShouldUnitThreatValuesBeSecret()` and test `issecretvalue` on the returned values, with a tank in the party. |
| What does ruleset do to data keys? | Realm and faction keying assumes realms; Forever has none, and the AH scope is unconfirmed. | Print realm APIs on characters in two rulesets; compare AH data and name formats; check whisper toasts with two-part names. |
| Which build, and when? | Launch is Nov 4 at 3 pm PST (Guild Order) but one outlet's metadata says Nov 5 ([Gamewave](https://gamewave.fr/world-of-warcraft-forever/wow-forever-les-restrictions-d-addons-de-midnight-seront-aussi-appliquees/)); beta ends Oct 21 or 22; "Camelot" TOC naming is expected to change ([developer guide](https://wowforeverguides.com/addons/developers)). | Not an in-game test: confirm against Blizzard's own post, then run `docs/SMOKE-TEST.md` on the last beta build, the launch build and before Dec 9. |
| Do the secure-snippet and aura reads work? | One tester reports missing `loadstring_untainted` on build 69893 (`WrapScript` throws); Vanguard falls back, but is unverified. | Run click casting, hover keys and bar paging on the current build; `/vg status`. |
| Does the client expose a new-quest flag, Legacy data or trainer lists? | No source says. | Dump quest and Legacy frame data sources; open a trainer and read what it lists. |

Two further conflicts are lower stakes. WeakAuras is "recommended" by Gfinity and "stalled" by ExitLag, so treat it as unavailable. DBM is a "limited port" per ExitLag, so verify the boss-timer docks with it loaded.

## Conclusion

The research changes the question from "what features are missing" to "what can be trusted at launch". The two cheapest items on the list, a settings guard and a data-key audit, protect the features Vanguard already has, and the most defensible new work is quest, spell and group tooling built on data the client gives you, because those are the areas where players are served by one-author addons and where the restrictions do not bite. Combat features should wait for the threat and snippet tests rather than for more research.

The weakest link is evidence, not engineering. Every demand claim here is supply-side, many guides are commercial or copy one another (Gfinity and [EpicCarry](https://epiccarry.com/blogs/?p=41550) share a list), and lifetime download counts blur flavors. A short alpha with 10 to 20 Forever players, and the first week of launch data, would rank this list better than any further desk research.
