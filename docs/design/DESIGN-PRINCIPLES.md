# Vanguard UI/UX review: design principles and element targets

Written 2026-10-17 against `main` at `8e249a4` (v0.6.0 plus the calendar and legacy-tracker merges), by the design-research thread of Cliff's full UI/UX review. Two sibling pieces of research cover what this one does not: the inventory of game frames Vanguard does not yet own, and the consistency audit of Vanguard's own widgets; they are the project's research files and land here as they are finished. The update plan reads all three, so this file is written for that reader: each element has a target spec in Vanguard's own vocabulary (theme tokens from `docs/THEME.md`, primitives from `Core/Theme.lua`, rows from `Core/Controls.lua`), a "Vanguard today vs target" note where the code or the walkthrough shots show the gap, and a cost guess. Section 5.1 lists every new token, constant and primitive the targets name, so nothing below is a loose idea.

**Nothing here was checked in a Forever client.** Every "today" claim comes from the code at `8e249a4` (file:line) or from Cliff's real in-game walkthrough shots (project files, not in the repo; taken on 0.5.0 and 0.6.0; the shot number is cited), and every one of those claims was re-checked against the code and the images by a second pass before this was delivered. Every geometry figure is a target for an in-game check, not an observation; where the text says a thing "matches", it matches in code and in the headless harness, and the in-game look is still owed to the batch checklist (#284 or its successor). The rules in `CLAUDE.md` still bind: colours and sizes live only in `Core/Theme.lua`; every fade is listed in `docs/THEME.md`; nothing third-party without a licence note and Cliff's yes. **Principles only: no other project's code, art, profiles or text is copied.**

Decisions already made and respected here: square corners are the default with the rounded toggle (`T.CORNER_RADIUS` 4, PR #355); eight dark variants and no light theme; faction look defaults to crest only (#389); pixel snapping and text tiers are in `docs/design/VISUAL-POLISH.md` (built: #225 to #239, #257 to #259); the reskin plan in `docs/handoff/RESKIN-PLAN.md` is fully ticked (60 of 60). Cliff's asks from the character-pane thread tonight (branch `work/toggle-restyle`, not yet on origin as of this writing): pill switches with a round 3D knob in each theme's colours, sliders with a rounded track and a round thumb, a restricted font list, and no line above the active tab unless a better indicator sits on the tab's top-most edge. All four are treated below as decided: the switch is 2.5, the slider 2.8, the tab 2.3 and the font list 2.21, each with exact geometry.

## 0. The five findings that matter most

1. **"Selected" is said six different ways, and that is what makes the UI read as assembled rather than designed.** The options nav uses an accent rail plus an accent wash and an accent-tinted icon (`Core/OptionsWindow.lua:686-695`); game tabs use a 2 px accent bar on the outer edge (`T.TAB_BAR`, `Core/Theme.lua:1395`); segments use a solid accent fill with `void` text; dropdown items use `accentText` plus an accent check glyph; the character pane's selected side tab keeps the game's gold rim (shot 12); the AH Lists list uses a rail plus accent text (shot 29, where the accent was arcane). The target is one grammar with three cases (section 1.7): **choose-one-of-few** = filled accent capsule (segments, switch, radio), **navigate** = left rail in `accent` plus `raisedHover` fill with the text unchanged (nav, list selection), **tab** = the attached tab (section 2.3; window tabs and side tabs alike), no line. Everything that marks a current thing uses one of those three.
2. **Two yellows.** `gold` (`ffd100`, the game's) and the brass accent (`d9a441`) sit about 13 degrees apart in OKLCH hue (92.7 vs 79.8) and are both used as "important": brass for the current nav item, the switch, the logo; gold for the window title on most skinned game windows (`Talents`, `Dorin Songblade`, `Inbox`, `Lists`, `Combined Backpack`: shots 13, 14, 19, 29, 10; `Modules/Windows.lua:194` gives the title the `heading` role and keeps the game's colour), quest titles, money, the zone name, elite names. Vanguard's own windows title in `text` (`Quest log`, `Vanguard`, `Set up Vanguard`), and so does the character pane (shot 12, white). The target: `gold` only where the game gives it meaning (quest titles and "!" marks, money, elite, the leader's crown); every window title, Vanguard's or the game's, in the `title` role in `text`. This one change removes most of the "mismatched" feel at zero art cost, and it is where the X post's "mis-matching textures and fonts" has a point: the eye sees Friz-gold-on-parchment habits next to slate-and-brass ones.
3. **Half-skinned surfaces.** Inside slate windows the eye still finds parchment (the inbox, shot 19), painted talent backdrops (shot 13), the character pane's bronze-framed stats list, its gold-rimmed selected side tab and the bronze separators between the others (shot 12), the merchant's red "can't use" row plates (shot 14), and the game's gold-rimmed check boxes (there is no `T.CheckBox`; `Modules/WindowsSocial.lua:511` says the checkbox keeps the game's art). The sibling inventory thread lists the frames; the principle for the plan is **one material per window**: inside a `T.Panel` every inner surface is `inset`, `raised` or `panel`, every control is a theme primitive, and art is kept only where it *is* the content (portraits, icons, the talent tree's spell icons, the map).
4. **Controls are not on one grid.** Row controls are 36×18 (switch, `Core/Controls.lua:100-111`), 140×6 (slider track at −66 with a 52-wide value slot, `:398-409`, `:452-457`), 180×22 (dropdown, `:345-353`), label-width×20 (segments, `:171-173`, `:200-211`), 28×28 (swatch, `Core/Widgets.lua:44-54`); standalone buttons are 24 tall in dialogs (`Core/Dialog.lua:13-21`), 32-row in options cards, 24 for inputs. The right edge aligns (−8) but heights, widths and knob shapes do not. The target is one control scale (section 1.9): row 28, inline control height 20, standalone button height 26, control column 200 wide, every width and height a multiple of 4 except the 14 px thumb and badge discs, and every radius from the radius scale (0, 4, full). The pill switch and the round slider thumb Cliff asked for are the first two controls on that scale, so they set it.
5. **Depth has two languages.** Vanguard's own panels carry edge + sheen + bevel + shadow (`T.Panel`, `T.Button`), hover eases to `raisedHover` on buttons but is a white wash on rows and tabs, press is a 30 % `shade` overlay on buttons but an `accentDim` fill on the switch, the scroll thumb is a 3 px bar at 0.5 alpha in the options window (`Core/OptionsWindow.lua:636-644`) but a raised panel with an accent edge plus arrow buttons in skinned game windows (`T.ScrollBar`, `Core/Theme.lua:1492` on). The target (sections 1.6 and 1.7) is a written state matrix: **one hover (a `hover` wash on see-through things, `raisedHover` on opaque ones), one press (a `shade` overlay, 30 % on opaque controls and 20 % on rows, the label down 1 px on buttons), one disabled (`disabled` text, no hover), one focus ring (`T.FocusRing`)**, and one scrollbar look used by both the options window and skinned windows.

The ranked list of everything else is section 5; the questions only Cliff can answer are section 6.

## 1. What makes an interface read as one system

A professional UI is recognised less by any one control than by the player never noticing a seam: the same spacing, the same four surfaces, the same way of saying "this is the one you picked", the same 100 ms when something moves. Each principle below ends with what Vanguard does today and what the target is.

### 1.1 Hierarchy by value, not hue

The most important thing on a surface is the brightest; chrome is the darkest; colour (hue) carries meaning and nothing else. On a dark theme that means text tiers do the hierarchy (`text` > `text2` > `muted` > `disabled`, already Lc −93/−75/−57/−35 per VISUAL-POLISH) and the accent is spent on exactly one thing per view: the current item, the primary action, or the focus. The eye should land on the accent once and then on the brightest text.

- **Today:** the tiers are right (#227). The accent is spent several times per view: in the options window (shot 03) it marks the nav item (rail, wash and icon), the switch, the slider fill, the segment fill, the reset dot (`OptionsWindow.lua:95`) and the "Changed" dots (`:451`). The slider thumb is `white` (`Controls.lua:407`) and the search glyph `text2` (`:487`). The window title bar is quiet, which is right.
- **Target:** one accent-filled control per row at most; accent on the current nav item's rail only (no wash, icon in `text`: section 2.12); the switch's "on" is accent because it is a chosen state, but the slider's fill goes `borderHi` and the thumb stays `white` with a 1 px `accent` ring, so a page with six sliders is not six brass bars (**[DECIDE] 6.5**). The reset dot stays accent (it is an affordance). Rule for authors: *if a view has more than two accent fills at rest, demote the least important to `text2` or `borderHi`.*

### 1.2 A spacing scale

Everything sits on a 4 px scale with named steps, so two layouts written by two people line up: 4 (tight, inside a control), 8 (between related things, the default inset), 12 (between a label and its value column), 16 (window padding, between cards), 24 (between sections), 2 only between repeated cells of one control. Borders and sheens are screen pixels and never count toward the grid (`docs/THEME.md`, Spacing).

- **Today:** the THEME.md rule exists but there are no tokens; `OptionsWindow.lua:13` uses `PAD = 10`, cards inset 4, captions at 8, controls at −8, `Dialog.lua:36` titles at 12/−10, `Setup.lua` `PAD = 16`, nav icon at 14 and text at +9 (`OptionsWindow.lua:445-448`). Ten and nine are off-scale.
- **Target:** `T.SPACE = { tight = 4, gap = 8, col = 12, pad = 16, section = 24 }` in `Core/Theme.lua`, read by every layout; `PAD` 10 → 12 in the options cards (the card title's 18 px line stays), nav icon 14 → 12, icon-to-text 9 → 8. `T.PixelRound` wraps every value so the grid survives fractional scales. Cheap: a dozen constants and one visual re-check.

### 1.3 A type scale that is small, and used the same way everywhere

Five sizes are enough for a window: title 15, heading 13, body 12, small 10, badge 9 (today's roles already map onto that, #258; THEME.md spreads 17 roles over those five sizes, the outlined world and bar roles included). What makes type read as one system is the *pairing* of size, weight and colour per role, applied identically in every module: section headers always upper-case `small` in `muted`; captions always `label` in `text`; values always `number` in `text2` unless they carry meaning; window titles always `title` in `text`.

- **Today:** roles are right, pairing is not: skinned game windows title in `heading` (13, keeping the game's colour, gold on most of them, white on the character pane: `Windows.lua:194`, shots 12, 13, 14, 19), Vanguard's own in `title` (15, `text`); card titles are `small` `muted` upper-case in options (`OptionsWindow.lua:184-188`, which upper-cases the string) but `heading` `text` sentence-case in the quest log and AH (`Lists`, `Deals 485 found`, shot 29); the AH column headers are `small` `text2` with the sorted one in `accentText` plus an arrow (`Modules/AuctionTable.lua:84-93`, `:114`); the bag section headers are `cell` (11 SemiBold) in `accentText` (shot 10). Tooltips use the game's font objects at 14/12/11 (right), chat at 12.
- **Target:** one pairing table in `docs/THEME.md` ("Type pairings") that names, for each *place* (window title, section header, column header, caption, value, hint, badge), the role and colour token, and a quality check that greps `T.Text(..., "heading"` calls against it. Decisions to make there: section headers in Vanguard windows become upper-case `small` `muted` everywhere (the options look; the upper-casing moves from the string to a role flag, a proposed addition to `T.ROLES`), column headers `small` `text2` in sentence case, bag sections `small` `text2` (not the accent).
- **Casing:** sentence case for everything but section headers. No trailing colons on captions (`Health:` in the character stats is the game's; Vanguard's own captions never carry one).

### 1.4 Colour roles and text tiers

A surface has a fill, a border, a sheen and text; a state has a fill and a glyph. Vanguard's token set already separates these (surfaces, text, state, reaction, resources, dispel, casts, healing, action buttons, xp/rep, faction, zone). The surface and text tokens this file leans on, with their default values and OKLCH lightness (computed from the hex; the roles are THEME.md's):

| Token | Default | L | Role in this file |
|---|---|---|---|
| `inset` | `0c0e13` | 0.164 | control fills, wells, tracks |
| `panel` | `13161c` | 0.200 | windows, the HUD |
| `raised` | `1b1f26` | 0.238 | cards, menus, popovers, the knob tile |
| `raisedHover` | `262a32` | 0.284 | an opaque control under the mouse; a selected row's fill (new use) |
| `divider` | `2f343d` | 0.324 | lines between groups, segment separators |
| `borderHi` | `43484f` | 0.400 | the bevel's top line, the focus base, the demoted slider fill (new use) |
| `edge` | `000000` | — | every one-pixel border |
| `sheen` | white 0.06 | — | the line inside a top border |
| `shade` | black 0.5 | — | press overlays, bar backgrounds, the knob shadow (new use) |
| `hover` | white 0.08 | — | the wash on see-through things |
| `void` | `0b0d11` | — | text and marks on an accent fill |
| `text` / `text2` / `muted` / `disabled` | `e8eaee` / `c8cdd6` / `a6adb9` / `7c8390` | — | the four text tiers |
| `accent` / `accentText` / `accentDim` | `d9a441` / `e6b45b` / `8a6a2f` | — | fills and rails / accent text and rings / accent borders only |

Three rules keep it coherent:

1. **Accent is the only chrome hue.** State colours (`good`/`warn`/`bad`) and the game's colours (class, quality, reaction, `gold`) mark *content*; they never paint chrome (borders, fills of controls, rails). The one exception stays: a `bad` tile under the close button on hover (`T.CONTROL_TILES.close`, `Theme.lua:1288`), which is a convention players know.
2. **`gold` is content, not chrome.** See finding 2. Grep today: 40 uses of `"gold"` outside the theme; the content ones (money, quest titles, elite, leader crown, quest "!" on nameplates, AH prices) stay; the chrome ones move to `text`/`title` or `accentText`: the skinned windows' titles (`Windows.lua:194` keeps the game's colour), `GW.CREATION_HEADS` (`WindowsSocial.lua:444`), `GW.QUEST_HEADS` (`Windows.lua:316`), the report-cheating dialog title (`WindowsSystem.lua:43`, the only caller of that `heading()` is line 278), the map header glyph tint (`WindowsMap.lua:210`), `GW.ITEM_TEXT_TAGS` H1-H3 (`Windows.lua:62`) and the quest log's zone headers (`QuestLogView.lua:118`). Quest *titles* stay gold inside quest text (the game's convention and the one the tracker uses), but the window chrome around them does not.
3. **Colour is never the only signal.** Every state that colour marks also has a shape: a glyph (`T.STATE_GLYPHS`), a rail, a fill, a check, a ring. The dispel letters already do this; the rest of the UI should be read with that test.

Variant discipline: every variant must keep the same *roles* (a variant may not make `raised` darker than `panel`). `theme.test.mjs` checks contrast; add a check that `inset` < `panel` < `raised` < `raisedHover` < `divider` < `borderHi` in OKLCH L for every variant. Computed here from THEME.md's values: seven variants pass; **contrast breaks it** (`divider` `4a5260` is L 0.437, lighter than `borderHi` 0.400), and `raisedHover` and `borderHi` are not per-variant (`Theme.lua:80`), so in contrast the step from `raised` (0.200) to `raisedHover` (0.284) is twice the others. Both are small token fixes for the plan.

### 1.5 Radii

Three radii, no more: **0** (square, the default), **4** (`T.CORNER_RADIUS`, the rounded toggle: today panels and buttons only, `ROUNDED_KINDS` at `Theme.lua:953`; the new checkbox, the segment group and edit boxes join that list so a rounded profile has no square controls left on a rounded card), **full** (a capsule: the pill switch, the slider track and thumb, badges, the scroll thumb). The full radius is not part of the corners toggle: a switch is a pill in both modes because a pill is its shape, not its decoration, the way a radio button is round in every design system. Tabs, slots, status bars and tooltips stay square in both modes (already the rule in #355).

- **Today:** 0 and 4 exist (`CornerLine`/`CornerFill` at 8 of 32); nothing is a capsule yet; the switch knob is a 14×14 square, the slider thumb a 6×14 bar.
- **Target:** generated capsule parts in `Media/` (section 4.2, named in 5.1): quarter-disc fill and line pieces at radius 10 (the 20 px pill track) and radius 7 (the 14 px badge capsule), a 4-tall end cap at radius 2 (the slider track), full discs at 14 (thumb), 16 (knob, with its rim baked) and 20 (the knob's soft shadow and the slider's hover wash), each with a 2x copy for `pixelMultiple` 2, white RGB under alpha, a clear pixel inside the edge. Masks (`CreateMaskTexture`) are the alternative at radius 6 and up (VISUAL-POLISH item 12) and are expected to come out soft below that, which is why the pieces are baked: the pipeline Vanguard already tests (`scripts/media.test.mjs`) needs no client feature.

### 1.6 Borders and depth

One border language: a one-screen-pixel `edge` line around every surface, and depth from three things only, each tied to a surface tier:

| Tier | Fill | Border | Sheen | Bevel | Shadow | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Window (`panel`, alpha 0.94 over the world, 0.98 for dialogs and setup, 1.0 for the options window) | `panel` | `edge` | yes | no | yes (floating) | options, dialogs, skinned game windows, bags, tooltips |
| Card (`raised`) | `raised` | `edge` | yes | yes | only when it floats (popovers, the dropdown menu) | cards in a window, popovers, the dropdown menu |
| Control (`inset`) | `inset` | `edge` | yes on buttons and edit boxes, no on switch and slider tracks | yes on buttons, no on boxes and tracks | no | buttons, edit boxes, switch and slider tracks, segments at rest |
| HUD (`panel`, `T.HudPanel`) | `panel` | `edge` | no | no | only with `hudShadow` | unit frames, bars, minimap, tracker |

Nesting rule: a window holds cards; a card holds controls; a control never holds another surface. Never an `inset` inside an `inset` (a switch track inside an inset row), never a `raised` inside a `raised` at the same level (a raised dropdown button on a raised card is the exception that reads as a lifted control; keep it `inset`, the current `T.Button`, instead). Strata: windows on `DIALOG`, dialogs and menus on `FULLSCREEN_DIALOG` (today's `Dialog.lua:32`, `Controls.lua:295`), tooltips on `TOOLTIP`, and nothing of Vanguard's above a tooltip.

- **Today:** the table matches `T.Panel`/`T.Button`/`T.EditBox`/`T.HudPanel` (`Theme.lua:1023-1036`, `:1167-1189`). Gaps: `GW.Skin` paints game windows with `pad 6` (right) but their inner areas keep template art (finding 3); `W.Swatch` and `W.Primary` are `T.Button`s with a fill laid over the inset (the primary's accent fill covers the bevel: it should be its own tier, section 2.4).
- **Target:** this table goes in THEME.md as the rule; the inventory thread's list of inner game surfaces is painted to it.

### 1.7 State design: one matrix

| State | Opaque control (`T.Button`, dropdown, the segment group's frame) | See-through row (option rows, list rows, nav, tabs) | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rest | `inset` fill, `edge`, bevel | nothing | role colour |
| Hover | ease to `raisedHover` fill + `borderHi` top line (0.1 s in / 0.13 s out, `T.EaseHover`) | `hover` wash (white 8 %), instant | unchanged (never recolour text on hover; the fill says it) |
| Pressed | `shade` 30 % overlay + label down 1 px, instant (`T.PressButton`) | `shade` 20 % overlay, instant | unchanged |
| Selected / on | `accent` fill, `void` text or knob (`choose-one-of-few`) | `accent` rail 2 px on the left + `raisedHover` fill (`navigate`); or the attached tab (`tab`) | `accentText` only where there is no fill to carry it (a dropdown's current item, a link) |
| Disabled | no hover, no press, fill unchanged, text `disabled`, icons `disabled` at 1.0 alpha (never alpha-fade the whole control: it blurs the border) | same | `disabled` |
| Focus (keyboard) | `T.FocusRing`: 1 px `accentText` 2 px outside, 1 px `edge` gap | same | — |
| Locked (a slider that cannot change) | fill and caption `muted`, tooltip says why (`addLock`) | — | `muted` |

The three controls with a moving part keep the same vocabulary on their own geometry, and their element specs are the rule for them: the switch (2.5: hover brightens the knob, press drops it 1 px, the track never changes), the segments (2.7: `hover` wash on an unselected segment, press `shade` 20 %), the slider thumb (2.8: a `hover` wash disc behind the thumb, 12 % while dragging). Rules: every state is a *layer* over an unchanged base, so states stack (hover + selected shows both), and no state moves geometry except press (1 px). Two exceptions to unify: the switch's pressed look today (`accentDim` fill, `Controls.lua:76`) becomes the knob drop; the window controls' hover tile (`T.CONTROL_TILES.other`, `accent` at 0.35, `Theme.lua:1288`) is an accent hover, which the matrix otherwise forbids (**[DECIDE] 6.12**). The slot's accent-edge hover (`T.SLOT_STATES`, `Theme.lua:1195`) stays: on an action button the accent edge *is* the slot's border.

### 1.8 Motion

Motion in a combat UI has one job: to say *where a thing came from* so the eye is not startled. Durations: 100 ms in and 130 ms out for a hover, 130 ms for a window open (with the 4 px rise), 100 ms for a close, 150 ms cap in combat, and nothing longer out of combat except the AFK screen (0.4 s) and the cast bar's end fade (0.35 s). Easing OUT for things that appear, IN for things that leave (an `AnimationGroup` with `OUT` smoothing is what `T.FadeIn` uses). Nothing pulses at rest. A switch or slider that moves its knob does so instantly today; the target allows a 100 ms ease on the knob's travel out of combat (a `Translation` animation on the knob's own frame, which is never protected) and instant in combat or with Reduce motion (**[DECIDE] 6.7**).

- **Today:** `T.HOVER_IN/OUT` 0.1/0.13, `T.WINDOW_OPEN/CLOSE/RISE` 0.13/0.1/4, cast-bar fade 0.35, AFK 0.4/0.2, toasts 0.12 (`Theme.lua:1894`, `:1951`; all listed in THEME.md).
- **Target:** add the knob ease (one row in the fades table), and a rule that *menus and tooltips never animate* (they already do not).

### 1.10 Alignment grid

Two columns in every settings and detail view: captions left-aligned at the row's left inset, controls right-aligned at the row's right inset (today's layout, keep it). Values inside a control column align on their *right* edge (slider values do: `addValueText`; AH prices do). In tables, numbers right-aligned in `number`, text left-aligned, icons at a fixed 16 px column with 8 px to the text. Dialog buttons are right-aligned with the primary rightmost (today's `Dialog.lua`, keep), 8 px apart.

- **Today:** the setup wizard puts `Skip setup` at the far left and `Next` at the far right (shot 02); the quest dialog's `Goodbye` and `Continue` sit together at the right with `Continue` greyed (shot 15); the quest log's buttons (`Track`/`Untrack` as one toggle, `Share` only when the quest can be shared, `Abandon`, `Map`; `QuestLogView.lua:76`, `:171-175`) are a row (shot 16); the AH Lists' six are a 3×2 grid of equal widths (shot 29).
- **Target:** one rule: *primary at the right, secondary beside it, an escape or destructive action (Skip, Abandon, Goodbye) alone on the far left.* The setup wizard already does this; the quest dialog would move `Goodbye` left; the quest log would move `Abandon` left (**[DECIDE] 6.10**). Equal widths within a group.

### 1.11 Iconography

One family, already defined (THEME.md, Icons): 2-unit strokes on a 16 grid, silhouettes from game-icons for concepts, strokes for chrome. Add the rules that are still implicit: a glyph in a control is tinted by the control's text tier (`text2` at rest, `text` on hover and when its row or tab is selected, `accentText` only where the selected state has no fill to carry it, such as a dropdown's current item, `disabled` when disabled), never by an accent fill unless the whole control is selected; a glyph and its label are 8 px apart; a glyph never sits closer than 4 px to a border; every glyph used in a 20 px control is drawn at 12 or 16, never scaled from 32 (`T.Icon(name, size)` with `sizedIcons`, #232, is the mechanism and should become the default once checked in game).

### 1.12 Copy tone

Captions are names, not sentences: `Font`, `Text size`, `Corners`, `Window ornaments`. The explanation goes in the row's description (the module row already has `desc`) or a tooltip note (`O.SettingNote`), never in the caption after a colon, and "(reload)" leaves the caption for the note and the footer notice (which already exists: `Some changes need a reload`). Buttons are verbs (`Reset theme`, `Scan the auction house`, `Open all`: all fine). Empty states say what would fill them (`Nothing to show` → `No damage yet this fight`, section 2.19). Sentence case everywhere; a space before a unit, as THEME.md writes it (`70 % to 130 %`); the shipped slider shows `105%` with no space (shot 03), so the value formatter should add it.

- **Today (0.6.0, Cliff's screenshot of the options window):** `Color assist: friendly blue, neutral yellow, hostile vermilion (reload)`, `Theme variant: surfaces and text`, `Corners: square or rounded panels`, `Window ornaments: a brass rule or corners on large windows`, `Pixel-perfect scale (crisp 1-pixel lines)`, `Soft shadows under frames (reload)`. (`Faction look: crest and heading font` is the 0.5.0 caption in shot 03; 0.6.0 already says `Faction look`.)
- **Target:** `Color assist` + note; `Theme variant`; `Corners`; `Window ornaments` + note; `Pixel-perfect scale` + note; `Soft shadows under frames` + note. The search index keeps the long text (it indexes captions and notes), so nothing becomes harder to find.

## 2. Element by element

Each entry: the target (geometry in UI units at 100 % text size, snapped through `T.PixelRound`; one-pixel lines are screen pixels via `T.Pixel`), tokens, states, motion, what to avoid, and Vanguard today vs target with the file or shot that shows it. ### 2.3 Tabs and active-tab indication

Cliff's decision: no line above the chosen tab unless a more elegant indicator sits on the tab's top-most edge. `T.Tab` is `Theme.lua:1470`, its bar `T.TAB_BAR` and icon dim `T.TAB_DIM` at `:1395`, `paintTab` at `:1415-1424`; it handles the game's three tab templates (`PanelTabButtonTemplate`, `TabSystemButtonTemplate`, `LargeSideTabButtonTemplate`). Two targets that meet the decision, in order of recommendation (**[DECIDE] 6.3**):

**A. The attached tab (recommended).** The selected tab is drawn as part of the content panel: its fill is the panel's (`panel` for a window's main tabs, `raised` for tabs on a card), its border runs around three sides only, and the fourth side, the one touching the content, has *no* edge line, so tab and page read as one shape. Unselected tabs are `inset` with a full border and `text2` labels, pulled 1 px away from the content edge so the selected one visibly bridges the gap. Selected label `text`, SemiBold not needed (the fill carries it). Hover: `hover` wash on unselected tabs only. No accent anywhere on a tab. This is how every desktop OS has drawn tabs since the 1990s; it needs no colour, works in every variant, and reads at a glance because the *content* claims the tab. It is drawable with today's parts: `paintTab` already swaps `inset`/`raised`; the one new piece is `opts.open = "BOTTOM"` (or TOP/LEFT/RIGHT) on `T.Panel`, which skips that edge's line and the sheen on that side, plus a 1 px overlap of the tab onto the content panel's border. On a see-through window (alpha 0.94) the selected tab's fill is the window's fill at the window's alpha, so the two read as one surface; in rounded mode a tab stays square (the #355 rule) and only its two outer corners would round, so leave all four square. Side tabs (character pane, guild): the same rule rotated: the selected side tab has no edge on the side that touches the window; side tabs are 32×32 with the icon at 20 (the game's `LargeSideTabButtonTemplate` size stays, only its art changes). Overflow: the game lays its tab rows out; Vanguard's own windows never need more than five tabs (the AH has five).

**B. A top-edge indicator.** If a coloured mark is wanted anyway, it sits *on* the tab's outer edge: a 2 px `accent` line that *replaces* the tab's top border (not a bar inside it, not a line floating above), full tab width, square ends, and the tab's fill goes `raised`. This is Material's primary-tab indicator translated (M3 draws a 3 dp rounded indicator at the content side; on a dark slate UI the outer edge reads better because the window border is right there). It needs the current `placeBar` with a one-pixel outward shift so the line covers the edge pixel.

Geometry in both: tab height 24 (inline tabs) or 28 (window tabs), label padding 12 each side, min width 64, tabs 1 px apart (the border lines touch, no gap), the row 16 from the window's left edge.

- **Avoid:** the accent bar inside the tab (today's `T.TAB_BAR` on TOP) and any line "above" the tab; dimming unselected icons below `T.TAB_DIM` 0.55; bold on the selected label plus a fill (double emphasis).
- **Today vs target:** `T.Tab` paints `raised` + a 2 px accent bar, which is exactly the "awkward line" (shots 13, 14, 19, 29 show it on the outer edge, floating). The toggle-restyle thread is removing the line; this spec is what replaces it. Cost: M (an `open` edge option on `T.Panel`, a `paintTab` change, re-check the three templates in `scripts/tests/windows-tabs.test.mjs`).

### 2.4 Buttons

Four kinds, one shape: height 26 standalone (20 inline), min width 80, label padding 16, corners from the radius scale (0 or 4 with the toggle). All share the state matrix (1.7).

| Kind | Fill | Border | Label | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Secondary (default) | `inset` + bevel | `edge` | `label` in `text` | every ordinary action (`T.Button`) |
| Primary | `accent` | `edge` | `label` SemiBold in `void`, no shadow (the `clear` shadow colour `T.Text` already uses for `void`, `Theme.lua:1666`) | the one action a view is about: `Next`, `Import`, `Apply`, `Scan the auction house` (`W.Primary`) |
| Danger | `inset` + bevel | `edge` | `label` in `bad` | `Abandon`, `Delete`, `Reset…`; the fill never turns red (a red fill is an alert, not a button) |
| Icon | `raised` tile 20×20 at 0.9 | none | glyph 12 px `text2` | window controls (`T.WindowControl`); hover and press per 2.2 |

Hover for the primary: fill eases to `accentText` (lighter) over 0.1 s, not `raisedHover`; press: `shade` 30 % as for the rest. Disabled primary: fill `accentDim`, label `disabled`. Busy (a scan running): label swapped for its progress text, hover and press off, never a spinner.

- **Avoid:** two primaries in one view; a primary with a bevel line through its accent fill; button labels that are nouns; the game's `UIPanelButtonTemplate` art showing anywhere inside a skinned window.
- **Today vs target:** `W.Primary` (`Controls.lua:49-59`) lays an accent `fill` texture at BORDER −1 over a `T.Button`, so the inset's bevel lines draw over the accent (visible in shot 02's `Next`: a light line along its top); the danger kind does not exist (`Abandon` in shot 16 is a plain secondary). Cost: S (a `primary`/`danger` opt on `T.Button` that swaps the fill tier and label colour, and skips the bevel).

### 4.2 Translating those ideas to what WoW's frame API can draw

There is no CSS: no border-radius, box-shadow, transitions or vector strokes (the 12.1 `SetSVG`/VectorGraphics path is unconfirmed on Forever and stays an experiment, #239). Everything is a texture, a font string, an anchor and an animation group. The table maps each design-system idea to the Vanguard mechanism that draws it, and the pixel rule that keeps it crisp.

| Design-system idea | Frame API mechanism | Vanguard today | Pixel rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface tiers / elevation levels | three fills (`inset`, `panel`, `raised`) + a soft shadow ring; "tonal elevation" is a lighter fill, not a bigger shadow | `T.Panel` opts, `Media/Textures/Shadow` 8 pieces or one nine-slice (`SetTextureSliceMargins`, #236) | shadow reach rounded to whole pixels (`T.PixelRound`) |
| State layer (hover 8 %, pressed 12 %) | a white texture on the `HIGHLIGHT` layer (hover, free: the client shows it) or `OVERLAY` (pressed, scripted) at that alpha | `hover` (white 0.08), `shade` 0.3 press, `raisedHover` on opaque controls | the layer is `SetAllPoints` so it needs no snapping |
| Corner radius 4 | four quarter-arc line pieces + quarter-disc fills, edges stopped short (`CornerLine`/`CornerFill`) | `T.CORNER_RADIUS`, PR #355 | pieces drawn at 8 of 32 so one texel is one pixel at 4 px |
| Full radius (pill, disc) | baked discs and quarter-discs, or `CreateMaskTexture` with a circle mask (radius 6 and up only; masks are bilinear-filtered) | not built | bake at the display size and at 2x for `pixelMultiple` 2; white RGB under alpha (#228) |
| Drop shadow on a knob | a second, larger disc texture at `shade` alpha under the knob, offset 0,−1 | not built | its size is `T.PixelRound`ed |
| 1 px borders | four `WHITE8X8` strips sized `T.Pixel` thick | `T.Panel` edges | never rounded to whole units; snapping off (`T.Snap`) until #226 settles |
| Focus ring 2 px outside | eight strips: four `edge` (gap) + four `accentText` (ring) | `T.FocusRing` | placed at the frame's pixel size |
| Transition 100 ms ease-out | `AnimationGroup` with an `Alpha` or `Translation` animation, `SetSmoothing("OUT")`; `SetIsFrameBuffer` for a whole window (#239) | `T.FadeIn/Out`, `T.OpenWindow`, `T.EaseHover` | the end state is snapped (`T.WINDOW_RISE` lands on the grid); nothing scales |
| Knob travel | `Translation` on the knob's own frame (never a protected one), or re-anchor at the end | not built | end anchor snapped; instant in combat / Reduce motion |
| Gradient fills | `SetGradient("VERTICAL", CreateColor(...), CreateColor(...))` (10.0+); ≤ 2.4 % lightness or it bands | `T.PANEL_GRADIENT`, off by default | none |
| Text styles / type ramp | `SetFont(path, size, flags)` per role; shadow via `SetShadowOffset`/`SetShadowColor`; outline via flags; no tracking, no OpenType features (tabular digits baked at build) | `T.Text`, `T.ROLES`, `make-fonts.py` | sizes are whole points; at a pixel-perfect scale one point is one pixel |
| Icon sizing | one TGA per display size picked by `T.Icon(name, size)` (SDF-baked) | `sizedIcons`, off until checked | never scale a 32 px glyph to 12 |
| Disabled 38 % | recolour text to `disabled`, desaturate icons (`SetDesaturated`), keep alpha 1 (alpha-fading a bordered control blurs its edge) | `T.DisabledLook` | none |
| Nine-slice cards | `SetTextureSliceMargins(l, t, r, b)` + `SetTextureSliceMode(Enum.UITextureSliceMode.Stretched|Tiled)` on one texture (10.2+) | `sliceRings` for shadow and glow, off until checked | margins in texture pixels; the texture is drawn at its native scale at the corners |
| Alpha gradient on text (fade-out of overflow) | `FontString:SetAlphaGradient(start, length)` | not used | an alternative to `…` truncation in tables; feature-detect |
| Radial progress (a round cooldown ring) | `SetRadialProgressBar*` (12.1, unconfirmed on Forever) | not used | experiment only |

Three constraints to write into every spec: (1) a texture is drawn at its anchor's fractional position unless the anchor is snapped, so every *size and offset* passes through `T.PixelRound`/`T.PixelSize`, and odd/even parity is kept between a child and the parent it is centred on (VISUAL-POLISH item 4); (2) protected frames (action buttons, unit buttons, the reload overlay) take no anchor or size change in combat, so every control that lives on one keeps its animated parts on a Vanguard-owned child frame; (3) the `HIGHLIGHT` layer is free hover for any `Button` and costs no script, so hover washes go there and only hover *fills that ease* (`T.EaseHover`) need an animation.

## 6. Open questions for Cliff [DECIDE]

1. **[DECIDE] Window titles on skinned game windows:** (a) keep the game's centred placement but colour them `text` in the `title` role (recommended: no layout risk; the setup wizard centres its title too, so centred is not foreign to Vanguard), or (b) left-align them like the options window, the quest log and the dialogs (a layout change on every game window; the game re-anchors `TitleContainer` on some).
2. **[DECIDE] Slider track ends at 4 px tall:** (a) baked 2 px rounded ends (a 4-tall end cap; recommended, since the ask was a rounded track, and the cost is one more media piece), or (b) square ends (one pixel of difference at 1080p, crisper, but not what was asked).
3. **[DECIDE] Active tab:** (a) the attached tab, no colour (recommended), or (b) the 2 px accent line *on* the tab's top edge replacing the border. Both satisfy "nothing above the tab".
4. **[DECIDE] Nav selection:** (a) rail + `raisedHover`, icon and text in `text` (recommended, finding 1), or (b) keep the accent wash and accent-tinted icon (today's look, which players may already know from 0.6.0).
5. **[DECIDE] Slider fill colour:** (a) `borderHi` with the accent only on the thumb's ring (recommended), or (b) accent fill as today and as Cliff's slider reference shows (a one-token change either way).
6. **[DECIDE] Section headers outside the options window:** (a) upper-case `small` `muted` everywhere (recommended), or (b) `heading` sentence-case in windows that are lists (quest log, AH), keeping two styles on purpose.
7. **[DECIDE] Knob and thumb ease (0.1 s):** on by default out of combat, or off until checked in game? Recommended: build it behind the existing Reduce motion switch and check it in the next batch.
8. **[DECIDE] Checkbox mark style:** (a) `inset` box with an `accentText` check (recommended: many-of-many stays light), or (b) `accent`-filled box with a `void` check (matches the switch's on state more closely, but makes a list of checked boxes heavy).
9. **[DECIDE] A "class colour" accent preset:** every other suite offers it and players will ask. (a) add `class` to `T.PRESET_ORDER` (the accent follows `RAID_CLASS_COLORS` for the player's class, with `accentText`/`accentDim` derived as for any hex; the collision notes in THEME.md apply: a Warrior's tan sits near brass, a Shaman's blue near arcane), or (b) leave it out (recommended for launch: it makes the selection colour collide with class-coloured content, which 1.4 forbids, and the five presets plus any hex already cover it by hand).
10. **[DECIDE] Quest log buttons:** (a) `Abandon` alone at the far left, `Track`/`Share`/`Map` at the right (recommended: the 1.10 rule, and it keeps a destructive button away from the others), or (b) the four in a row as today.
11. **[DECIDE] Enter in dialogs:** (a) Enter presses the primary in the question, reload and text dialogs except when the primary is destructive (recommended), or (b) Enter does nothing (today).
12. **[DECIDE] Window-control hover tile:** the maximize/minimize and other controls hover to an `accent` tile at 0.35 today (`T.CONTROL_TILES.other`). (a) change it to `raisedHover` with the glyph `text`, so only the close button keeps a coloured tile (recommended: the state matrix), or (b) keep the accent tile as a deliberate exception.
