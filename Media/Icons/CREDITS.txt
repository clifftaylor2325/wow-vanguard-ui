Each icon's page is https://game-icons.net/1x1/<author>/<name>.html, with the
name in parentheses above split at its first dash.

Changes: the black background square is removed and each glyph is rasterized
to a 32x32 white image with a 2-pixel clear margin, whose alpha is the glyph,
so Vanguard can tint it.
