Painted panel textures

PanelSlate.tga, PanelVellum.tga  made by Cliff Hearthvale with Leonardo.ai (https://leonardo.ai) on a paid plan
in private mode, AI-generated, then turned into faint white tiles (grey, seamless, box-filtered, alpha-mapped) by
scripts/lib/media-painted.mjs. All rights reserved with the rest of Vanguard.

The other panel textures (PanelGrain, PanelStone, PanelLeather) and every other file in this folder are drawn by
Vanguard's own build scripts.
