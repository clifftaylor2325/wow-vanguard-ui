Make the suite deterministic under any order and give the weekly run a shuffled order with a printed seed (CI-03, roadmap criterion 3, D-05).

Decomposition note: this plan is horizontal CI and test-structure work with no production layer. Task 1 is the thinnest proof first: the one order-dependent file found by research (`scripts/link-wow.test.mjs`, 6 of 12 cases red when shuffled) passes under shuffled seeds.
